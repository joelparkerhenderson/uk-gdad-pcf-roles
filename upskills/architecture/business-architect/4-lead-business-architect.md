# Lead business architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead business architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are accountable for the outputs of a business architecture team, work with executive stakeholders, set direction on methods and tools, and develop the profession through coaching, mentoring and training. The resources below emphasise leadership, strategy, architecture maturity and capability building.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Strategic Leadership: Impact, Change, and Decision-Making](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/strategic-leadership-impact-change-decision-making): A Dartmouth Tuck specialisation with Sydney Finkelstein covering the mistakes smart executives make, the neuroscience behind effective decision making, and how to support the people around you. Aimed at people driving change in an organisation.

- [Strategic Leadership: Influence and Thought Leadership](https://www.coursera.org/learn/strategic-leadership-influence-thought-leadership): Covers high-level influence techniques used by senior leaders, including mapping influence networks, framing, building legitimacy through consistency, and leading meaning-making in uncertain moments.

- [TOGAF 10 Foundation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/togaf-10-foundation): Covers the Architecture Capability and architecture governance alongside the ADM. Relevant at lead level because you are responsible for the capability itself, not only its application.

## Udemy courses

- [Enterprise Architecture Practitioner with the TOGAF Standard](https://www.udemy.com/course/togaf-10-practitioner/): The practitioner-level treatment of applying TOGAF in a live organisation, including tailoring the ADM and establishing governance.

- [The Practice of Enterprise Architecture (Part II)](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-practice-of-enterprise-architecture-part-ii/): Based on empirical research into real architecture practice, covering how artefacts such as business capability models actually function as instruments of communication and decision.

- [BIZBOK Exam Prep: 1000+ Business Architecture Questions](https://www.udemy.com/course/bizbok-exam-prep-1000-business-architecture-questions/): A large practice question bank across the Business Architecture Body of Knowledge, useful for leads setting a common standard of knowledge across their team.

## Skillsoft courses

- [TOGAF Advanced: Enterprise Architecture](https://www.skillsoft.com/courses/5557912-togaf-advanced-enterprise-architecture/): An in-depth treatment of the framework covering how to enhance and mature architectures, directly relevant to raising the maturity of a practice.

- [Courses for Leader as Coach](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/leader-as-coach-abbca300-c400-11e7-bfa9-ef5e1a5e569f): Covers the qualities of an effective coach, developing a trusting coach and coachee relationship, and implementing coaching best practice. Supports the coaching and mentoring expectations of the role.

- [Courses for Strategic Thinking](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/strategic-thinking-1b49aa30-e719-11e6-9835-f723b46a2688): Covers the vision and persuasion skills strategic leadership requires, which differ from those of day-to-day operational leadership.

- [15.317 Organizational Leadership and Change](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-317-organizational-leadership-and-change-summer-2009/): A Sloan course blending theory and practice, in which students reflect on prior leadership experiences and apply the lessons to develop their leadership capability further.

- [15.067 Competitive Decision-Making and Negotiation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-067-competitive-decision-making-and-negotiation-spring-2011/): Develops negotiation skill through integrative bargaining cases between two and more than two parties over multiple issues. ## Research articles

- [TOGAF-based Enterprise Architecture Practice: An Exploratory Case Study](https://aisel.aisnet.org/cais/vol43/iss1/20/): Demonstrates that organisations adapt EA methodologies rather than adopting them wholesale, and that actual practice diverges substantially from published methodology. Essential context before mandating a standard.

- [Nuanced Responses to Enterprise Architecture Management: Loyalty, Voice, and Exit](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07421222.2018.1550549): Analyses how people respond to architecture management using loyalty, voice and exit, providing a vocabulary for diagnosing organisational resistance.

- [Achieving Enterprise Architecture Benefits: What Makes the Difference?](https://tunguska.home.xs4all.nl/Publications/Docs/Achieving%20Enterprise%20Architecture%20Benefits%20-%20Steenbergen,%20Foorthuis.pdf): Van Steenbergen and Foorthuis examine the conditions under which architecture practice actually delivers value, rather than assuming it does.

- [A theory building study of enterprise architecture practices and benefits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-014-9542-1): A 293-respondent study showing benefits flow through intermediate results, most notably compliance with EA and architectural insight, which has direct implications for how a lead sets objectives.

- [Dynamic enterprise architecture capabilities and organizational benefits: an empirical mediation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10036): Examines how dynamic architecture capabilities mediate between architecture work and organisational benefit.

- [The theoretical basis of enterprise architecture: A critical review and taxonomy of relevant theories](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0268396220977873): Kotusev and Kurnia's review and taxonomy of the theories underpinning the discipline.

- [Challenges of Government Enterprise Architecture Work: Stakeholders' Views](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-85204-9_31): Concludes from 21 government interviews that the governance level of architecture must be adequately adjusted and enforced as a tool for business operations development.

- [Enterprise Architecture and IT Governance to Support the BizDevOps Approach: a Systematic Mapping Study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-024-10473-2): Maps 86 primary and 11 secondary studies, noting the field's shortage of empirical validation and prevalence of recommendation-oriented work.

- [Enterprise Architecture in Smart Cities: Developing an Empirical Grounded Research Agenda](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10630732.2022.2122681): Positions architecture as a means of communication between stakeholders to improve service delivery, with a public-sector-adjacent evidence base.

- [Stakeholder Perception of Enterprise Architecture](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220757098_Stakeholder_Perception_of_Enterprise_Architecture): Finds that stakeholders frequently perceive architecture management as abstract, and links governance and communication practices to stakeholder value perceptions.

## Professional development tips

- Define what good looks like for your team in writing, then hold reviews against it rather than against personal taste
- Measure the practice by decisions influenced and misalignments corrected, not by artefacts produced
- Build a community of practice that outlives any individual, including onboarding material and reusable patterns
- Develop your executive communication so that a complex enterprise view fits on one page and one minute
- Invest in succession by giving your strongest architects genuine accountability, not just harder tasks
- Learn enough about funding, portfolio and commercial processes to make roadmaps actionable
- Keep a live view of where the organisation is strategically misaligned, and raise it before it becomes a delivery failure
