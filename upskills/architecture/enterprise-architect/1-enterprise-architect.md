# Enterprise architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of enterprise architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you support other architects in developing the strategy, network and communicate with stakeholders across domains, use best practice and emerging technologies, and learn to identify influences and risks. The resources below emphasise framework fundamentals, modelling technique and the evidence base for architecture practice.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [TOGAF 10 Foundation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/togaf-10-foundation): Covers the distinctions between TOGAF 9.2 and TOGAF 10, the core concepts of enterprise architecture, the Architecture Development Method, and how to apply architecture governance principles involving Architecture Boards, Contracts and Compliance.

- [IBM Systems and Solutions Architect Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-systems-and-solutions-architect): A multi-course programme covering architectural thinking, requirements, architecture decisions and documentation practice, connecting enterprise-level direction to deliverable solution designs.

- [Enterprise Data Architecture and Operations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/enterprise-data-architecture-and-operations): Covers enterprise architecture principles including the TOGAF and Zachman frameworks, alongside data modernisation and lifecycle management.

## Udemy courses

- [Implement Enterprise Architecture Step by Step](https://www.udemy.com/course/implement-enterprise-architecture-step-by-step/): Works through enterprise architecture with TOGAF, Zachman and FEAF, developing business, information, application and technology architectures plus governance, change management and real-world case studies.

- [Enterprise Architecture Foundation with the TOGAF Standard](https://www.udemy.com/course/togaf-10-ea-foundation/): A foundation-level treatment of the TOGAF standard covering the ADM, the architecture content framework and the Enterprise Continuum.

- [Enterprise Architecture Modeling Foundation with ArchiMate 3](https://www.udemy.com/course/archimate-3/): Covers the strategy layer, linking capabilities to outcomes, and how internal and external behaviour elements realise value streams and capabilities. ArchiMate is the standard notation for making architecture models precise and shareable.

- [Enterprise Architecture: Architectural Principles and Patterns](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/enterprise-architecture-architectural-principles-patterns-7ad56361-c387-4163-9ed6-571890b250b1): An intermediate course exploring architecture concepts and the principles that should be followed when designing architecture for applications and systems.

- [Architecture Development Method (ADM) Cycle](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/architecture-development-method-adm-cycle-d4cd2224-292d-11e7-9844-0242c0a8080b): A phase-by-phase treatment of the TOGAF ADM, covering the inputs, steps and outputs of each phase.

- [Courses for DDAT Enterprise Architect](https://www.skillsoft.com/journey/ddat-enterprise-architect-9cfc71da-ce69-4df8-b22b-fd3f0ac06429): A curated learning journey mapped to the UK Government Digital and Data profession capability framework for this exact role.

## edX courses

- [Introduction: Emerging Technologies in an Enterprise Environment (UBCx)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/university-of-british-columbia-introduction-emerging-technologies-in-an-enterprise-environment): Teaches Open Group enterprise architecture standards and terminology across technology, data and application domains, and how to explain an organisation's architecture in the context of TOGAF.

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (Institute of Science Tokyo)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/institute-of-science-tokyo-introduction-to-business-architecture-3): Covers mapping a business architecture using the DEMO methodology and Enterprise Ontology, giving a formal counterweight to looser modelling habits.

- [Enterprise Data Management (Indiana University)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-administration/indiana-university-enterprise-data-management): Covers data strategy and governance, relational databases and SQL, data integration, master data management and big data technologies, filling in the data domain of an enterprise architecture.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [ESD.34 System Architecture](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/esd-34-system-architecture-january-iap-2007/): Covers the principles of architecting complex systems, including decomposition, interfaces, and the relationship between form and function. A rigorous foundation that generalises well beyond software.

- [15.571 Generating Business Value from Information Technology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-571-generating-business-value-from-information-technology-spring-2009/syllabus/): Examines how IT enables four fundamental business operating models, using case studies including UPS, Merrill Lynch, 7-Eleven Japan and Pacific Life.

- [1.264J Database, Internet, and Systems Integration Technologies](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/1-264j-database-internet-and-systems-integration-technologies-fall-2013/): Covers data modelling, UML, relational databases and SQL alongside internet technologies including XML, web services and service-oriented architectures.

## Research articles

- [TOGAF-based Enterprise Architecture Practice: An Exploratory Case Study](https://aisel.aisnet.org/cais/vol43/iss1/20/): Kotusev's case study showing that organisations adapt EA methodologies to their own needs rather than adopting them out of the box, and that real practice differs substantially from published methodology.

- [A theory building study of enterprise architecture practices and benefits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-014-9542-1): A 293-respondent survey developing a model in which EA does not deliver benefits directly but operates through intermediate results, most notably compliance with EA and architectural insight.

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Argues that many EA benefits are intangible and realised indirectly within business change projects, and that the advisory services enabled by the architecture provide the benefit.

- [Dynamic enterprise architecture capabilities and organizational benefits: an empirical mediation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10036): Examines how dynamic architecture capabilities mediate between architecture work and organisational benefit.

- [The theoretical basis of enterprise architecture: A critical review and taxonomy of relevant theories](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0268396220977873): Kotusev and Kurnia review the theoretical foundations of the field and classify the theories used to explain architecture practice.

- [Stakeholder Perception of Enterprise Architecture](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220757098_Stakeholder_Perception_of_Enterprise_Architecture): Finds that stakeholders frequently perceive architecture management as abstract and difficult to comprehend, and links governance processes and communication to stakeholder values.

- [Enterprise Architecture and Organizational Benefits: A Case Study](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/19/8237): An open-access case study tracing how architecture practice produced measurable organisational outcomes.

- [How enterprise architecture leads to organisational benefits](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401222000883): Traces the mechanisms connecting architecture work to organisational outcomes, in the International Journal of Information Management.

- [Enterprise Architecture and IT Governance to Support the BizDevOps Approach: a Systematic Mapping Study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-024-10473-2): Maps 86 primary and 11 secondary studies, noting a shortage of empirical validation and a prevalence of recommendation-oriented papers.

- [Challenges of Government Enterprise Architecture Work: Stakeholders' Views](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-85204-9_31): An empirical study in Finnish state government concluding that the governance level of architecture must be adequately adjusted and enforced as a tool for business operations development.

## Professional development tips

- Learn one framework well enough to know which parts your organisation actually uses and which it ignores
- Build a personal network across domains, because opportunities for improvement usually appear at the seams between them
- Practise writing architecture decision records, since a recorded rationale is more valuable than a diagram
- Track emerging technology deliberately, with a short note on what problem each one would solve for your organisation
- Ask to be included in risk conversations early, so you learn to spot influences and risks before they mature
- Take a small piece of the strategy and trace it end to end into delivered systems, to see where it breaks down
- Get feedback from delivery teams on whether your outputs helped them, and act on what they tell you
