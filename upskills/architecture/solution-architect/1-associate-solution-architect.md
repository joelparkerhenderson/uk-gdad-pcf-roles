# Associate solution architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of associate solution architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you work on solution architecture with support and guidance, learning the methods, patterns and tools of the discipline and contributing to solutions led by others. The resources below emphasise architecture fundamentals, common patterns and their trade-offs.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [AWS Cloud Solutions Architect Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/aws-cloud-solutions-architect): Teaches how to design architectural solutions on AWS, covering solution and software architecture, cloud infrastructure and scalability, and preparing for the AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate exam.

- [Architecting Solutions on AWS](https://www.coursera.org/learn/architecting-solutions-on-aws): Uses case-based scenarios to identify services and features for building resilient, secure and highly available solutions, walking through architecture design decisions and their trade-offs.

- [TOGAF 10 Foundation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/togaf-10-foundation): Covers the Architecture Development Method, architecture governance, and stakeholder management. Useful for connecting individual solution designs to the enterprise architecture that governs them.

## Udemy courses

- [Software Architecture and Design of Modern Large Scale Systems](https://www.udemy.com/course/software-architecture-design-of-modern-large-scale-systems/): A practical course on architecting real systems that scale to millions of daily users and process large volumes of data.

- [Mastering Software Architecture Patterns and System Design](https://www.udemy.com/course/mastering-software-architecture-patterns-for-system-design/): Explores hexagonal, clean and onion architectures alongside distributed models including microservices, service-oriented architecture and event-driven systems, plus API gateways, CQRS, event sourcing, containers and serverless.

- [Design Microservices Architecture with Patterns and Principles](https://www.udemy.com/course/design-microservices-architecture-with-patterns-principles/): Traces the evolution from monolithic to event-driven microservices, designing for high availability, high scalability, low latency and resilience to network failure.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [ESD.34 System Architecture](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/esd-34-system-architecture-january-iap-2007/): Covers principles and methods for technical system architecture, including resolving ambiguity to identify system goals and boundaries, the creative process of mapping form to function, and the analysis of complexity through decomposition and re-integration.

- [6.005 Elements of Software Construction](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-005-elements-of-software-construction-fall-2008/): Covers the fundamentals of building correct, understandable software, including specification, abstraction and testing. Strong grounding for architects who review other people's designs and code.

- [16.842 Fundamentals of Systems Engineering: System Architecture and Concept Generation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-842-fundamentals-of-systems-engineering-fall-2015/resources/session-4-system-architecture-and-concept-generation/): A focused session on generating and comparing architectural concepts rather than settling on the first workable one.

## Blog posts

- [The Up-and-Running Guide to Architectural Fitness Functions](https://mikaelvesavuori.se/blog/2023-08-20_The-Up-and-Running-Guide-to-Architectural-Fitness-Function): A practical guide to writing fitness functions that give objective assessment of architecture characteristics, with concrete implementation examples.

- [Architecture Fitness Functions](https://synchronium.github.io/software-architecture-wiki/concepts/fitness-functions.html): Explains the concept and its origin in evolutionary computation, and lists the forms a fitness function can take, from unit tests and ArchUnit rules to chaos engineering runs and manual review.

- [Using Cloud Fitness Functions to Drive Evolutionary Architecture](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/using-cloud-fitness-functions-to-drive-evolutionary-architecture/): Applies fitness functions to cloud architecture specifically, with implementable examples.

- [Microservices as an Evolutionary Architecture](https://www.thoughtworks.com/insights/blog/microservices-evolutionary-architecture): Argues that microservices are best understood as one way of supporting guided, incremental change rather than as an end in themselves.

- [Architecture Fitness Functions: Catch Drift Before Prod](https://architectviewmaster.com/blog/architects-dont-skip-leg-day-fitness-functions-matter): Focuses on catching architectural drift automatically before it reaches production.

- [Building Evolutionary Architecture: book takeaways](https://philodev.one/posts/2024-09-evolutionary-architecture/): A practitioner's summary of the key ideas of evolutionary architecture, useful as an orientation before reading the book itself.

- [Agentic Fitness Functions: Extending Evolutionary Architecture Beyond Deterministic Rules](https://www.infoq.com/articles/agentic-fitness-functions-evolutionary-architecture/): Explores using AI agents calibrated against architecture decision records to assess architecture characteristics that resist deterministic checking.

- [Architecting the Future: Enterprise Architecture Strategies for Agile and Resilient Organizations](https://www.rsaconference.com/library/blog/architecting-the-future-enterprise-architecture-strategies-for-agile-and-resilient-organizations): Discusses how architecture practice supports agility and resilience together.

- [12 Enterprise Architecture Principles to Future-Proof IT](https://www.superblocks.com/blog/enterprise-architecture-principles): Sets out architecture principles with reasoning, useful when your solution designs must justify themselves against organisational principles.

- [5 steps for building collaboration into an enterprise architecture revamp](https://www.redhat.com/architect/architecture-refresh-leadership): Practical guidance on collaborative architecture work, which matters when a solution crosses several delivery teams.

## Research articles

- [Identifying architectural technical debt, principal, and interest in microservices: A multiple-case study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0164121221000650): Based on 25 interviews with practitioners in seven large companies, this study identifies 16 architectural technical debt issues, their negative impact, and common repayment solutions with costs.

- [Microservices, Continuous Architecture, and Technical Debt Interest: An Empirical Study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/326059322_Microservices_Continuous_Architecture_and_Technical_Debt_Interest_An_Empirical_Study): A case study of an organisation migrating from a monolith to microservices, finding that total technical debt grew much faster in the microservices system, probably because refactoring was postponed.

- [The technical debt gamble: A case study on technical debt in a large-scale industrial microservice architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S016412122500216X): A mixed-method study combining static analysis with developer focus groups and interviews with the lead architect.

- [Exploring the evolution of technical debt in monolithic and hybrid microservice architecture: An industrial case study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0164121226000658): Provides empirical observations relevant to organisations considering migration from monolithic to microservice architecture.

- [Temporal Evolution of Architectural Complexity and Technical Debt in Microservices: An Exploratory Case Study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-12089-2_18): Examines the relationship between architectural complexity indicators, including declared dependencies, API endpoints, inter-service communication and size, and technical debt over time.

- [TOGAF-based Enterprise Architecture Practice: An Exploratory Case Study](https://aisel.aisnet.org/cais/vol43/iss1/20/): Shows how organisations adapt architecture methodologies in practice, and how far real practice diverges from the published method.

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Argues that architecture benefits are realised indirectly through advisory services within change projects rather than through the architecture artefacts themselves.

- [A theory building study of enterprise architecture practices and benefits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-014-9542-1): A 293-respondent survey showing that architecture works through intermediate results, most notably compliance and architectural insight.

- [Stakeholder Perception of Enterprise Architecture](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220757098_Stakeholder_Perception_of_Enterprise_Architecture): Finds that stakeholders often perceive architecture as abstract and difficult to comprehend, and links governance and communication practice to perceived value.

- [Enterprise Architecture and IT Governance to Support the BizDevOps Approach: a Systematic Mapping Study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-024-10473-2): Maps the literature on architecture and governance in continuous delivery settings, where solution architecture must keep pace with deployment.

## Professional development tips

- Learn to write a design that another engineer can build from without needing to interview you
- Study the trade-offs behind common patterns rather than memorising the patterns themselves
- Ask to review real production systems, because the gap between the design and the running system is where you learn most
- Keep architecture decision records for every non-obvious choice, including the options you rejected
- Build fluency in at least one cloud platform to a level where you can estimate cost and failure modes
- Sit with the delivery team through a release, so that operability stops being an abstraction
- Take feedback on your designs from operations and security colleagues early, not at a final gate
