# Associate business architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of associate business architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are applying architectural methods to produce outputs, breaking down complexity, managing stakeholder expectations, and beginning to provide advice and recommendations. The resources below emphasise applied modelling technique, consultancy skills and stakeholder relationship management.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [TOGAF 10 Foundation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/togaf-10-foundation): Covers the Architecture Development Method in depth, including the Architecture Vision and Business Architecture phases, plus stakeholder management, risk management, and architecture alternatives and trade-offs. The stakeholder and trade-off material is directly applicable at associate level.

- [Enterprise Data Integration, Governance and Architecture](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/data-architect-advanced): A specialisation covering enterprise data architecture, integration patterns and governance. Useful because business architects are frequently asked to reconcile the business view of information with how data is actually held.

- [IBM Systems and Solutions Architect Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-systems-and-solutions-architect): Covers architectural thinking, requirements elicitation, architecture decision records and documentation practice. Strengthens the craft of producing clear, defensible architecture outputs.

## Udemy courses

- [Business Architecture Foundation with the TOGAF Standard](https://www.udemy.com/course/togaf-10-ba-foundation/): Covers defining what a business does through a TOGAF 10 aligned business capability map, with detailed guidance on naming, description, layering, heat maps and value-stream mapping. Includes over 200 pages of material and two 40-question practice tests.

- [Enterprise Architecture Modeling Foundation with ArchiMate 3](https://www.udemy.com/course/archimate-3/): Covers the strategy layer including value streams and capabilities, and shows how internal and external behaviour elements realise them. ArchiMate is the notation most commonly used to make business architecture models precise and shareable.

- [The Practice of Enterprise Architecture (Part II)](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-practice-of-enterprise-architecture-part-ii/): Discusses business capability models as a subtype of Visions within the wider set of enterprise architecture artefacts, based on empirical research into real practice rather than framework theory.

## Skillsoft courses

- [Enterprise Architecture: Architectural Principles and Patterns](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/enterprise-architecture-architectural-principles-patterns-7ad56361-c387-4163-9ed6-571890b250b1): An intermediate course exploring architecture concepts and the principles that should be followed when designing architecture. Helps an associate move from following principles to articulating why they exist.

- [Architecture Development Method (ADM) Cycle](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/architecture-development-method-adm-cycle-d4cd2224-292d-11e7-9844-0242c0a8080b): A detailed treatment of the TOGAF ADM cycle phase by phase, including the inputs, outputs and steps of each phase.

- [Enterprise Business Architecture: The Formal Link between Strategy and Results (book)](https://www.skillsoft.com/book/enterprise-business-architecture-the-formal-link-between-strategy-and-results-78c22a70-efa2-11e6-9877-0242c0a80506): Explains the approach needed to develop a formal but pragmatic enterprise business architecture, emphasising the importance of architecture in reaching business goals and the behaviours and discipline needed to realise its potential.

## edX courses

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (Institute of Science Tokyo)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/institute-of-science-tokyo-introduction-to-business-architecture-3): Teaches mapping a business architecture using DEMO (Design and Engineering Methodology for Organizations) as explained in Enterprise Ontology. The formality of DEMO is a useful counterweight to looser capability mapping habits.

- [Introduction: Emerging Technologies in an Enterprise Environment (UBCx)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/university-of-british-columbia-introduction-emerging-technologies-in-an-enterprise-environment): Covers business architecture development and The Open Group Business Architecture, and helps explain an organisation's enterprise architecture in the context of TOGAF standards.

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (TokyoTechX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-administration/tokyo-institute-of-technology-introduction-to-business-architecture): The TokyoTechX run of the same material, covering enterprise ontology and business architecture mapping concepts.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.320 Strategic Organizational Design](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-320-strategic-organizational-design-spring-2011/): Covers when to use functional, divisional or matrix organisations, how IT creates new organisational possibilities, and innovative organisational forms including democratic decision making and crowd-based organisations. Gives an associate a wider design vocabulary than the org chart.

- [15.571 Generating Business Value from Information Technology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-571-generating-business-value-from-information-technology-spring-2009/syllabus/): Focuses on how IT enables four fundamental business operating models, using case studies to examine the trade-off between business process integration and standardisation.

- [15.769 Operations Strategy](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-769-operations-strategy-fall-2010/pages/calendar/): Discusses enterprise and value chain architecture and operations strategy, exploring business processes and strategic decision making. Strong material for reasoning about how work actually gets done at scale.

## Blog posts

- [The Business Architect's toolbox: Value Stream Mapping](https://bizzdesign.com/blog/business-architect-toolbox-stream-mapping): Argues that value stream mapping is one of the most powerful tools in a business architect's arsenal, because visualising how value flows across processes, teams and systems reveals inefficiencies and aligns capabilities.

- [Mastering Business Architecture Value Streams for Enterprise Value](https://www.capstera.com/business-architecture-value-streams/): Explains how value streams and capability maps are complementary, with capability maps defining what an organisation can do and value streams showing how those capabilities are orchestrated to deliver value.

- [Business Architecture Essentials: Aligning your Capabilities, Processes and Business Information](https://processrenewal.com/business-architecture-essentials-aligning-capabilities-processes-business-information-connecting-dots/): Connects the three core model types and shows how to keep them consistent with each other, a common failure point at associate level.

- [Value Streams, Capabilities, and IT Services: A Simple Model View](https://medium.com/enterprise-solution-architecture/value-streams-capabilities-and-it-services-a-simple-model-view-3b3e16738449): A practitioner article proposing a simple model linking value streams, capabilities and the IT services that support them.

- [Customer Journeys, Value Streams and Business Capabilities, and how to bring them together](https://scottmillett.medium.com/customer-journeys-value-streams-and-business-capabilities-and-how-to-bring-them-together-7eb912937e23): Scott Millett explains how three commonly confused artefacts relate, and how to use them together without duplicating effort.

- [Business Capabilities and Value Streams: Essential elements for Business Architecture](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/business-capabilities-value-streams-essential-elements-bib-shukla): A practitioner overview of the two central artefacts and the decisions each one supports.

- [SAFe and Business Architecture](https://scaledagile.com/blog/safe-and-business-architecture/): Explains how business architecture fits into a scaled agile delivery environment, which matters when architecture outputs need to feed agile planning cycles.

- [Business Architect: Skill Sets, Career Paths and Must-Haves](https://www.leanix.net/en/wiki/ea/business-architect): Sets out the combination of strategic analysis, systems thinking and modelling technique the role needs, and argues communication skill is the most crucial because architects engage across all levels of seniority.

- [Business Architect Role: Skills, Deliverables, and Value](https://capstera.com/knowledge-hub/articles/business-architect): Describes typical deliverables including the business capability map, value stream maps, capability heatmaps, target operating model designs and capability-based roadmaps that link strategic goals to funded initiatives.

- [The Power of Enterprise Architecture Operating Model](https://www.boc-group.com/en/blog/ea/operating-models-in-business-transformation/): Explains the role of operating models in business transformation and how architecture teams use them to structure change.

## Research articles

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Argues that many EA benefits are intangible and achieved indirectly within business change projects, and that it is the advisory services enabled by the architecture, rather than the architecture itself, that provide benefits. A useful corrective for architects who over-invest in artefacts.

- [A theory building study of enterprise architecture practices and benefits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-014-9542-1): A 293-respondent survey study showing that EA operates through intermediate results, most notably compliance with EA and architectural insight, rather than delivering benefits directly.

- [Dynamic enterprise architecture capabilities and organizational benefits: an empirical mediation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10036): An empirical mediation study of how dynamic architecture capabilities translate into organisational benefits.

- [Modeling Business Capabilities in Enterprise Architecture Practice: The Case of Business Capability Models](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372098424_Modeling_Business_Capabilities_in_Enterprise_Architecture_Practice_The_Case_of_Business_Capability_Models): Identifies nine general business capability modelling approaches used in practice for the purpose of aligning business and IT.

- [Designing Consistent Business Capability Models: A Method for Aligning Value Streams, Capabilities, and Information Concepts](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-94590-8_28): A design science method for keeping business architecture models consistent with each other, addressing a known gap in systematic alignment methods.

- [TOGAF-based Enterprise Architecture Practice: An Exploratory Case Study](https://aisel.aisnet.org/cais/vol43/iss1/20/): Kotusev's case study showing that organisations adapt EA methodologies to their needs rather than using them out of the box, and that actual practice often differs substantially from the published methodology.

- [Aligning Enterprise Systems Capabilities with Business Strategy: An extension of the Strategic Alignment Model using Enterprise Architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050918317332): Augments the Strategic Alignment Model with TOGAF to show how enterprise systems capabilities can be aligned to business strategy.

- [Stakeholder Perception of Enterprise Architecture](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220757098_Stakeholder_Perception_of_Enterprise_Architecture): Finds that stakeholders frequently perceive architecture management as abstract and difficult to comprehend, and links governance processes and communication to stakeholder values.

- [Enterprise Architecture and Organizational Benefits: A Case Study](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/19/8237): An open-access case study tracing how architecture practice produced measurable organisational outcomes in a specific organisation.

- [How enterprise architecture leads to organisational benefits](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401222000883): Traces the specific mechanisms by which architecture work translates into organisational outcomes, published in the International Journal of Information Management.

## Books on Amazon

- [Business Architecture: A Comprehensive Guide](https://us.amazon.com/Business-Architecture-comprehensive-Jonathan-Hunsley-ebook/dp/B0D2V1F5N5): By Jonathan Hunsley, Debra Paul and colleagues. - [The Practice of Enterprise Architecture: A Modern Approach to Business and IT Alignment](https://www.amazon.com/Practice-Enterprise-Architecture-Approach-Alignment/dp/0645082538): By Svyatoslav Kotusev. The canonical Business Model Canvas guide, useful for framing an organisation before detailed modelling.

- [Business Architecture: The Enabler of Business Strategy](https://us.amazon.com/dp/B0D8C7VWYQ): By Samuel Holcman. A methodology from the Business Architecture Center of Excellence focused on strategy enablement.

- [Essential Strategy and Enterprise Architecture](https://www.amazon.com/Essential-Strategy-Enterprise-Architecture-Haloedscape/dp/1507840144): A compact treatment of how strategy formulation and architecture practice connect.

## Professional development tips

- Practise producing one artefact end to end, from stakeholder interviews through to a reviewed and published model
- Learn a modelling notation such as ArchiMate well enough to use it precisely, not decoratively
- Test every model against a real decision it should support, and discard anything that supports no decision
- Build your consultancy muscle by writing short, clear recommendation papers rather than long analyses
- Deliberately practise facilitating a workshop, including handling disagreement in the room
- Seek out cross-cutting problems, because spotting them is a defining skill at this level
- Ask senior architects to critique your reasoning rather than your diagrams
