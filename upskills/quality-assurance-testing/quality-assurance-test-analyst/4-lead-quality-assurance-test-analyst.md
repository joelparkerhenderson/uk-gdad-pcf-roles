# Lead quality assurance test analyst - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead quality assurance test analyst.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you lead quality assurance across a service or programme, set the test strategy, and represent quality in release decisions. The resources below emphasise strategy, shared tooling and organisational influence.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

- [The Ultimate Guide to Quality Assurance Testing Metrics](https://testlio.com/blog/qa-metrics/): Covers metrics that evaluate the whole quality management process rather than individual test runs, starting from test coverage.

- [Exploratory Testing in Agile Teams](https://www.globalapptesting.com/blog/the-critical-role-exploratory-testing-plays-in-agile-teams): Explains the benefits including rapid feedback in early development and comprehensive bug detection without predefined scripts.

- [TDD vs BDD: Full Comparison](https://katalon.com/resources-center/blog/tdd-vs-bdd): Explains that test-driven development is developer-centric and code-focused while behaviour-driven development is user-centric and promotes collaboration through a domain-specific language.

## Research articles

- [Benefits and limitations of automated software testing: Systematic literature review and practitioner survey](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/6228988/): Combines a systematic review with a survey of 115 professionals, finding benefits in reusability, repeatability, coverage and execution effort, against high initial investment in setup, tool selection and training.

- [Test automation maturity improves product quality: quantitative study of open source projects](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0164121222000280): An empirical study of 37 open source Java projects using continuous integration, finding higher automation maturity associated with higher product quality and shorter release cycles.

- [The Impacts of Test Automation on Software's Cost, Quality and Time to Market](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1877050916001277): Examines three projects and finds positive effects on cost, quality and time to market, providing evidence for the business case.

- [An Analysis and Survey of the Development of Mutation Testing](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1109/tse.2010.62): Jia and Harman's comprehensive survey of mutation testing as a fault-based technique studied over three decades.

- [Using Mutation Analysis for Assessing and Comparing Testing Coverage Criteria](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/1703390/): Andrews, Briand, Labiche and Namin on empirically assessing test techniques across block, decision, C-use and P-use criteria.

- [The Effects of Continuous Integration on Software Development: a Systematic Literature Review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05451): Analyses 101 empirical studies covering quality assurance alongside development activities, defects and build patterns.

- [Recent Advances in Regression Testing Techniques](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0065245816300286): Surveys regression test selection, test case prioritisation and test suite minimisation, and the heuristics behind each.

- [Which process metrics can significantly improve defect prediction models? An empirical study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11219-014-9241-7): Identifies the process metrics that genuinely improve defect prediction, published in Software Quality Journal.

- [Empirical Study of Software Defect Prediction: A Systematic Mapping](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-8994/11/2/212): Maps defect prediction literature from 1995 to 2018 across 156 studies, finding lines of code and coupling metrics to be the strongest reported predictors.

- [Test Code Review in the Era of GitHub Actions: A Replication Study](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15935): Examines how test code is reviewed differently from production code and what that means for test quality.

## Professional development tips

- Set the test strategy across a service or programme and review consistently against it
- Judge the function by defects prevented and risks retired, not by tests executed
- Build automation and tooling as shared infrastructure rather than per-team scripts
- Develop testers deliberately, including their technical and their domain knowledge
- Represent quality in governance and release decisions with evidence rather than opinion
- Work with security, accessibility and performance specialists so that testing is genuinely whole
- Retire test activity that no longer finds anything, so the effort stays honest
