# Lead frontend developer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead frontend developer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you lead the frontend work of a team, set standards, and take accountability for accessibility and performance across your services. The resources below emphasise technical leadership, accessibility accountability and team development.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Strategic Leadership: Impact, Change, and Decision-Making](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/strategic-leadership-impact-change-decision-making): A Dartmouth Tuck specialisation with Sydney Finkelstein covering the mistakes smart executives make, the neuroscience behind effective decision making, and how to manage and support the people around you.

- [Strategic Leadership: Influence and Thought Leadership](https://www.coursera.org/learn/strategic-leadership-influence-thought-leadership): Covers high-level influence techniques used by senior leaders, including mapping influence networks, strong framing, building legitimacy through consistency, and leading meaning-making in uncertain or crisis moments.

- [Influencing the Organisation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/influencing-the-organisation-leading-within-and-beyond-the-organisation): Covers managing stakeholders and organisational politics while mobilising people, and aligning your mission with the organisation's purpose.

## Udemy courses

- [Front-End Web Development with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React](https://www.udemy.com/course/frontend-web-development-bootcamp/): Covers HTML5 semantic markup, forms, media elements and accessibility; CSS3 layouts, flexbox, grid and responsive design; modern JavaScript; and React components, state, props and hooks.

- [Become a Front-End Developer: HTML, CSS and Bootstrap](https://www.udemy.com/course/become-a-front-end-developer/): Includes web performance and accessibility topics such as lazy loading, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation and load-time improvement.

- [The Web Developer Bootcamp](https://www.udemy.com/course/the-web-developer-bootcamp/): A large, frequently updated course covering semantic elements including main, nav, section, article, aside, header and footer to improve accessibility and search visibility.

## Skillsoft courses

- [Courses for Clean Coding](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/clean-coding-8575b9b6-5528-42d1-ba5c-0e4bf0700582): Covers the philosophies, principles and practices that make software more efficient and less error prone, starting from what clean code is and why it matters.

- [Courses for Agile for Software Development](https://www.skillsoft.com/journey/agile-for-software-development-43d01c60-e3ca-4e12-8d32-d3fc3a2a693a): A proficiency journey from Agile and Scrum awareness through best practice for requirements and design, into how Agile methods are implemented in development and testing.

- [Clean Coding Techniques: Introduction to SOLID](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/clean-coding-techniques-introduction-to-solid-b857f940-ecad-11e7-8181-d3c41c82a4f4): A focused treatment of the five SOLID principles with examples of each being followed and violated.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.317 Organizational Leadership and Change](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-317-organizational-leadership-and-change-summer-2009/): A Sloan course blending leadership theory and practice, in which participants reflect on prior leadership experience and apply the lessons to develop their capability further.

- [15.067 Competitive Decision-Making and Negotiation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-067-competitive-decision-making-and-negotiation-spring-2011/): Develops negotiation skill through integrative bargaining cases between two and more than two parties over multiple issues.

## Blog posts

- [We've made the GOV.UK Design System more accessible](https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/2019/07/29/weve-made-the-gov-uk-design-system-more-accessible/): A concrete account of the accessibility changes made to a widely used component library and why.

- [GOV.UK Design System Team posts on the accessibility blog](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/author/gov-uk-design-system-team/): Ongoing posts from the team maintaining the components most UK government frontends are built from.

- [11 barriers to coding in the open and how to overcome them](https://technology.blog.gov.uk/2018/11/05/11-barriers-to-coding-in-the-open-and-how-to-overcome-them/): The common objections to working in public and how each is answered.

- [Technology in government: Open Standards](https://technology.blog.gov.uk/category/open-standards/): Covers how open standards are chosen and applied, which shapes frontend technology choices.

## Research articles

- [Accessibility engineering in web evaluation process: a systematic literature review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10209-023-00967-2): Reviews accessibility evaluation practice from 2010 to 2021, finding that 80 per cent of studies relied on automated tooling alone, 8 per cent involved users, and 12 per cent used hybrid approaches.

- [How compliance with web accessibility standards shapes the experiences of users with and without disabilities](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1071581922001756): Empirically examines whether standards compliance actually improves experience, which is the question that matters behind conformance scores.

- [Large-scale study of web accessibility metrics](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10209-022-00956-x): Compares accessibility metrics at scale and examines what they do and do not capture.

- [Optimising the website accessibility conformance evaluation methodology](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3493612.3520452): Proposes improvements to WCAG-EM, the standard methodology for determining conformance.

- [Automated evaluation of accessibility issues of webpage content: tool and evaluation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-92192-5): Presents and evaluates an automated accessibility tool, and is honest about the limits of automation.

- [Colour Contrast on the Web: A WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliance Audit of Common Crawl's Top 500 Domains](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.24067): A large-scale audit finding colour contrast to be the most common failure, consistent with the WebAIM Million findings.

- [Accessibility Analysis of Educational Websites Using WCAG 2.0](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3696318): Finds that most evaluated sites followed less than half the guidelines, a useful corrective to assumptions about baseline compliance.

- [Empirical Studies on Web Accessibility of Educational Websites: A Systematic Literature Review](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9092982/): Reviews the empirical accessibility literature and the methods used across it.

- [On the effects of continuous delivery on code quality: A case study in industry](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0920548921000830): A twelve-month industrial case study of what continuous delivery adoption did to code and product quality.

- [The Effects of Continuous Integration on Software Development: a Systematic Literature Review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05451): Analyses 101 empirical studies across development activities, quality assurance, defects and build patterns.

## Books on Amazon

- [Web Accessibility Cookbook: Creating Inclusive Experiences](https://www.amazon.com/Web-Accessibility-Cookbook-Inclusive-Experiences-ebook/dp/B0D74TJF5B): By Manuel Matuzovic. Written for anyone who writes frontend code, with a strong focus on HTML and on JavaScript-powered components such as toggles, accordions, modals, filters and navigation.

- [Web Accessibility by Design: Inclusive Front-End Patterns and UI Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/Web-Accessibility-Design-Inclusive-Guidelines-ebook/dp/B0FMJSVY2D): Covers semantic HTML and ARIA roles, accessible forms and navigation, colour contrast and motion control, with actionable techniques.

- [Web Design with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and jQuery Set](https://www.amazon.com/Web-Design-HTML-JavaScript-jQuery/dp/1118907442): By Jon Duckett. An unusually clear visual introduction to the core frontend technologies, still a good foundation.

- [Refactoring: Improving the Design of Existing Code](https://www.amazon.com/Refactoring-Improving-Existing-Addison-Wesley-Signature-ebook/dp/B07LCM8RG2): By Martin Fowler. The reference catalogue of refactorings, with substantial JavaScript examples in the second edition.

- [Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship](https://www.amazon.com/Clean-Code-Handbook-Software-Craftsmanship-ebook/dp/B0FPFZTZYY): By Robert C. Martin. Widely read guidance on readable, maintainable code, worth reading critically.

- [The Pragmatic Programmer: Your Journey to Mastery, 20th Anniversary Edition](https://www.amazon.com/Pragmatic-Programmer-journey-mastery-Anniversary-ebook/dp/B07VRS84D1): By Thomas and Hunt. Broad practical advice on the craft and on building professional judgement.

- [Continuous Delivery: Reliable Software Releases through Build, Test, and Deployment Automation](https://www.amazon.com/Continuous-Delivery-Deployment-Automation-Addison-Wesley-ebook/dp/B003YMNVC0): Humble and Farley on deployment pipelines and the practices that make frequent release safe.

- [Test Driven Development: By Example](https://www.amazon.com/Test-Driven-Development-Kent-Beck-ebook/dp/B095SQ9WP4): By Kent Beck. The original and clearest account of the technique.

- [Fundamentals of Software Architecture: A Modern Engineering Approach](https://www.amazon.com/Fundamentals-Software-Architecture-Engineering-Approach-ebook/dp/B0F1BWQGYZ): Richards and Ford on architecture characteristics and trade-offs, applicable to frontend architecture too.

- [Threat Modeling: Designing for Security](https://www.amazon.com/Threat-Modeling-Designing-Adam-Shostack-ebook/dp/B00IG71FAS): By Adam Shostack. Frontends handle user data and authentication; this is the standard reference for reasoning about that risk.

## Professional development tips

- Set frontend standards through automated checks, shared components and review rather than documents
- Own the accessibility position of your services, including the accessibility statement and its accuracy
- Balance hands-on work with enabling others, and be honest about the split
- Develop people deliberately, including those who will become better than you
- Work with design and content colleagues so that the front end is a shared responsibility
- Protect the team's ability to do quality work when delivery pressure rises
- Keep enough hands-on practice to remain credible with the developers you lead
