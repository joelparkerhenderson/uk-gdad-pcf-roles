# Senior business analyst - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of senior business analyst.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you take responsibility for the analysis of a whole service or change, challenge how problems are framed, and guide less experienced analysts. The resources below emphasise complex analysis, traceability and coaching.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Business Analysis: Process Modeling and Requirements Gathering](https://www.coursera.org/learn/business-analysis-process-modeling-requirements-gathering): Covers the BABOK framework alongside advanced process modelling with BPMN and swimlane diagrams, and managing functional and non-functional requirements with prioritisation techniques such as MoSCoW and Kano.

- [IBM Business Analyst Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-business-analyst-professional-certificate): A multi-course programme covering the analysis lifecycle, stakeholder engagement, requirements documentation and the tools business analysts use day to day.

- [Business Analysis: Key Definitions and Strategy Analysis](https://www.coursera.org/learn/business-analysis-mastery): Covers strategy analysis, identifying business needs, evaluating capabilities and constraints and defining achievable outcomes.

## Udemy courses

- [BCS Foundation Certificate in Business Analysis Exam Preparation](https://www.udemy.com/course/bcs-foundation-business-analysis-exam-prep-900-questions/): Covers the strategic context for business analysis, the POPIT model, PESTLE analysis, investigation techniques, requirements engineering and stakeholder analysis, with a large practice question bank.

- [Business Analysis: Process Modelling](https://www.udemy.com/course/business-analysis-process-modelling-foundation/): Teaches process modelling from the ground up so that you can construct models that accurately reflect business requirements.

## Research articles

- [Empirical research on requirements quality: a systematic mapping study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00766-021-00367-z): Screened 6,905 articles down to 105 primary studies, finding that research concentrates on improvement techniques while evidence-based definitions of quality attributes remain scarce.

- [The State-of-Practice in Requirements Elicitation: An Extended Interview Study at 12 Companies](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.11556): Documents what elicitation actually looks like in practice across a dozen organisations, rather than what textbooks prescribe.

- [The Effects of Requirements Elicitation Issues on Software Project Performance: An Empirical Analysis](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-319-05843-6_21): Traces causal relationships between elicitation problems and project outcomes, supporting priority setting.

- [A Practical Guide to Requirements Elicitation Techniques Selection: An Empirical Study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/267942937_A_Practical_Guide_to_Requirements_Elicitation_Techniques_Selection_-An_Empirical_Study): Examines how project context should influence which elicitation technique you choose.

- [A systematic literature review about software requirements elicitation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/314361585_A_systematic_literature_review_about_software_requirements_elicitation): Reviews the elicitation literature and the evidence behind the common techniques.

- [Ensuring quality in software requirement engineering process: A comparative study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1110866525001471): Reviews 57 articles from 2018 to 2023 and surveys industry practice against the quality practices identified.

- [Requirements Quality Research: a harmonized Theory, Evaluation, and Roadmap](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.10355): Proposes a coherent theory of requirements quality, addressing the field's fragmentation.

- [Understanding how agile teams reach effectiveness: A systematic literature review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053482224000469): Reviews the evidence on what makes agile teams effective, which shapes how analysis work is best organised.

- [Do Agile Scaling Approaches Make A Difference? An Empirical Comparison of Team Effectiveness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06599): Surveys over fifteen thousand team members and finds only minor differences between scaling frameworks.

- [Stakeholder Perception of Enterprise Architecture](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220757098_Stakeholder_Perception_of_Enterprise_Architecture): Finds that stakeholders often perceive analytical models as abstract and hard to comprehend, a warning worth heeding.

## Professional development tips

- Take responsibility for the analysis of a whole service or change, not individual requirements
- Challenge the framing of a request when the stated problem is not the real one
- Coach less experienced analysts on questioning technique rather than on documentation format
- Build relationships across policy, operations and technology so that analysis can lead to decisions
- Make your analysis traceable, so that a decision can be explained months later
- Work with architecture and data colleagues so that models are consistent across the organisation
- Keep close to users and frontline staff, since that is where the real process lives
