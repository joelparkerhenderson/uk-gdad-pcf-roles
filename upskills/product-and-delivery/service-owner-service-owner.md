## Research Articles

### 1. Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function
**Source:** ScienceDirect (2023)

**Summary:** A systematic literature review of empirical academic research into ITSM implementations published between 2012 and 2021. The research argues that ITSM forms a Management Control System (MCS), and found that IT managers implement a variety of management controls. The study provides comprehensive insights into how organizations implement and benefit from ITSM frameworks.

### 2. Impact of IT Service Management Frameworks on the IT Organization
**Source:** Springer - Business & Information Systems Engineering (2011)

**Summary:** An international survey of 491 firms was conducted to assess the benefits of the IT Infrastructure Library (ITIL). Results indicate that as the maturity of implementation increases, the perception of challenges decreases, and the number of realized benefits increases, as well as the number of implemented ITIL processes. The study demonstrates the value of mature ITIL implementations.

### 3. Stakeholder management in complex product systems
**Source:** ScienceDirect

**Summary:** An empirical study of a European district development megaproject examined stakeholder management in complex product systems contexts, deriving nine practices and four rationales that describe the engagement and disengagement of external stakeholders, and developing a processual model of stakeholder management. This research provides practical frameworks for managing stakeholders in complex environments.

### 4. IT Governance, IT Service Management and the Organizing Role of ITIL
**Source:** ResearchGate

**Summary:** This paper defines IT governance as the preparation for, making of and implementation of IT-related decisions regarding goals, processes, people and technology on a tactical or strategic level. It explores the intersection of governance and service management, providing frameworks for decision-making in IT organizations.

### 5. Measures for Benefits Realization
**Source:** Sheffield Hallam University (2017)

**Summary:** This analytical literature review is concerned with metrics and the measurement of outcomes in benefits realization. The research involved 127 relevant academic sources and analyzed 260 documents total. The study provides comprehensive guidance on establishing metrics and KPIs for measuring benefits realization.

### 6. Product Lifecycle Management for Complex Product Systems
**Source:** ScienceDirect (2021)

**Summary:** This paper presents the first ever framework for implementing Product Lifecycle Management within high value Engineering to Order programmes. When implementing PLM, the organisation should first understand its strategic objectives and core processes and use this to decide on the PLM approach. The research provides practical implementation guidance for PLM initiatives.

### 7. Strategic alignment and its impact on decision effectiveness
**Source:** ResearchGate

**Summary:** A comprehensive research study investigated the impact of strategic alignment on decision effectiveness, with results revealing that strategic alignment significantly and positively affects decision effectiveness and its dimensions. The research demonstrates the importance of alignment between strategy and execution.

### 8. User-Centred Design without Involving Users
**Source:** Taylor & Francis

**Summary:** A longitudinal case study examined an HCD-mature industrial company with a high focus on design and user-centeredness, exploring how human-centered design can be applied even when direct user involvement is not possible. The research provides insights into alternative approaches to user-centered design.

### 9. Implementing sustainable operational excellence in organizations
**Source:** Taylor & Francis

**Summary:** This research investigates how to implement sustainable operational excellence initiatives in organizations. The study builds a model for sustainability of operational excellence which considers social, economic and environmental aspects, and organizational culture and agility are found to contribute a major role in sustainable organizational excellence.

### 10. Transformational Leadership and Employees' Reactions to Organizational Change
**Source:** Journal of Applied Behavioral Science (2021) - Meta-Analysis

**Summary:** This meta-analysis examined numerous studies testing the linkage between transformational leadership and employees' reactions to organizational change. The study highlights the value of incorporating transformational leadership theory into change reaction literature. Research found that transformational leadership raises followers' commitment to change, which promotes innovative behavior.
