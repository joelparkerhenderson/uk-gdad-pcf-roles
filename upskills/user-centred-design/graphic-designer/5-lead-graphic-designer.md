# Lead graphic designer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead graphic designer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you lead graphic design across teams, set visual standards, and own the visual identity as it applies to services. The resources below emphasise design leadership, standards and organisational influence.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Strategic Leadership: Impact, Change, and Decision-Making](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/strategic-leadership-impact-change-decision-making): A Dartmouth Tuck specialisation with Sydney Finkelstein covering the mistakes smart executives make, the neuroscience behind effective decision making, and how to manage and support the people around you.

- [Strategic Leadership: Influence and Thought Leadership](https://www.coursera.org/learn/strategic-leadership-influence-thought-leadership): Covers high-level influence techniques used by senior leaders, including mapping influence networks, strong framing, building legitimacy through consistency, and leading meaning-making in uncertain or crisis moments.

- [Influencing the Organisation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/influencing-the-organisation-leading-within-and-beyond-the-organisation): Covers managing stakeholders and organisational politics while mobilising people, and aligning your mission with the organisation's purpose.

## Udemy courses

- [Figma Prototyping: A Deep Dive for UX and UI Designers](https://www.udemy.com/course/figma-prototyping-a-deep-dive-for-ux-ui-designer/): Covers basic prototypes, smart animation, reusable interactive components and interactions using variables and conditional logic.

- [Figma Advanced Course: Design, Prototyping and Responsive UI](https://www.udemy.com/course/figma-advanced-course-design-prototyping-responsive-ui/): Covers component architecture, variants and properties for reusable design systems, and using variables for colour, typography and spacing including light and dark modes.

- [Level Up Your UI/UX: Interactive Design and Prototyping in Figma](https://www.udemy.com/course/level-up-your-uiux-interactive-design-prototyping-figma/): Covers prototyping interface elements and building complex interactions such as sliders, accordions and drag and drop.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.317 Organizational Leadership and Change](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-317-organizational-leadership-and-change-summer-2009/): A Sloan course blending leadership theory and practice, in which participants reflect on prior leadership experience and apply the lessons to develop their capability further.

- [15.067 Competitive Decision-Making and Negotiation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-067-competitive-decision-making-and-negotiation-spring-2011/): Develops negotiation skill through integrative bargaining cases between two and more than two parties over multiple issues.

## Blog posts

- [GOV.UK Design System accessibility strategy](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/accessibility/accessibility-strategy/): Sets out how the Design System follows the four WCAG principles, which constrains colour, contrast and typographic choices in government work.

- [We've made the GOV.UK Design System more accessible](https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/2019/07/29/weve-made-the-gov-uk-design-system-more-accessible/): A concrete account of visual design changes made for accessibility and the reasoning behind each.

- [GOV.UK Design System Team posts on the accessibility blog](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/author/gov-uk-design-system-team/): Ongoing posts from the team maintaining the visual language most government services use.

- [GOV.UK Design System: Patterns](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/): Best practice solutions for specific tasks and page types, with the visual conventions government users now expect.

- [Content design: planning, writing and managing content](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/content-design/writing-for-gov-uk): The GOV.UK guidance based on research into how people read online, which shapes how visual and written content work together.

- [6 lessons academic research tells us about making data visualizations](https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2015/6-lessons-academic-research-tells-us-about-making-data-visualizations/): Summarises the perception evidence, including the finding that embellished charts were recalled better without loss of accuracy.

- [Perception in data visualization](https://rstudio-pubs-static.s3.amazonaws.com/368845_9c1cc6de9d984f5688872fadd719c9b4.html): Reviews how visual encodings rank for accuracy of judgement, following Cleveland and McGill.

- [Content accessibility: speak plainly](https://stormid.com/blog/content-accessibility-part-1-speak-plainly/): Connects clarity to accessibility, which applies to visual communication as much as to words.

- [How to make a user journey map](https://designnotes.blog.gov.uk/2016/04/21/how-to-make-a-user-journey-map/): From the head of design at the Government Digital Service, on producing visual artefacts that are actually used.

- [Unlocking Accessibility: Frontend developers discuss semantic HTML and accessible code](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/2025/04/25/unlocking-accessibility-frontend-developers-discuss-semantic-html-and-accessible-code/): Useful context on how visual designs become accessible implementations.

## Research articles

- [The Science of Visual Data Communication: What Works](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/15291006211051956): Franconeri, Padilla, Shah, Zacks and Hullman synthesise the evidence on what makes visual communication succeed, published in Psychological Science in the Public Interest.

- [A Survey of Perception-Based Visualization Studies by Task](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.07477): Organises the perception literature by the task the viewer is performing, which is the useful way to choose a visual encoding.

- [Eye of the Beholder: Towards Measuring Visualization Complexity](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.05536): Proposes ways to measure how complex a visual representation is for a viewer.

- [Visual Perception and Pre-Attentive Attributes in Oncological Data Visualisation](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12292122/): An applied study of pre-attentive attributes and how well-designed visuals exploit the high sensory bandwidth of vision.

- [Colour Contrast on the Web: A WCAG 2.1 Level AA Compliance Audit of Common Crawl's Top 500 Domains](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.24067): A large-scale audit finding colour contrast to be the most common accessibility failure, which is a visual design failure.

- [How compliance with web accessibility standards shapes the experiences of users with and without disabilities](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1071581922001756): Tests empirically whether standards compliance improves experience for real users.

- [Accessibility engineering in web evaluation process: a systematic literature review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10209-023-00967-2): Finds most accessibility evaluation relies on automated tooling alone, missing the judgement-based visual issues.

- [Data visualization in AI-assisted decision-making: a systematic review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/communication/articles/10.3389/fcomm.2025.1605655/full): Reviews 127 papers on how visual representation affects decisions.

- [Exploring the Effect of Plain Terminology on Processing and Comprehension of Administrative Texts](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ijal.12650): A reading experiment on comprehension of administrative text, relevant wherever visual design carries dense information.

- [Sample size in usability studies](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/2133806.2133824): Useful when planning testing of visual designs, since the familiar answer of five participants is often wrong.

## Books on Amazon

- [The Visual Display of Quantitative Information](https://www.amazon.com/Visual-Display-Quantitative-Information/dp/1930824130): By Edward Tufte. The foundational text on the theory and design of information graphics, with detailed analysis of 250 examples.

- [Data Visualization: A Practical Introduction](https://www.amazon.com/Data-Visualization-Introduction-Kieran-Healy/dp/0691181624): By Kieran Healy. Grounds visual choices in perception research rather than in taste.

- [Storytelling with Data: A Data Visualization Guide for Business Professionals](https://www.amazon.com/Storytelling-Data-Visualization-Business-Professionals-ebook/dp/B016DHQSM2): By Cole Nussbaumer Knaflic. The most widely used practical guide to making a visual carry an argument.

- [Web Accessibility Cookbook: Creating Inclusive Experiences](https://www.amazon.com/Web-Accessibility-Cookbook-Inclusive-Experiences-ebook/dp/B0D74TJF5B): By Manuel Matuzovic. Covers how visual decisions become accessible or inaccessible in implementation.

- [Web Accessibility by Design: Inclusive Front-End Patterns and UI Guidelines](https://www.amazon.com/Web-Accessibility-Design-Inclusive-Guidelines-ebook/dp/B0FMJSVY2D): Covers colour contrast, motion control and visual presentation alongside markup.

- [About Face: The Essentials of Interaction Design](https://www.amazon.com/About-Face-Essentials-Interaction-Design/dp/1118766571): Cooper, Reimann, Cronin and Noessel. Connects visual decisions to behaviour and to user goals.

- [Content Design](https://www.amazon.com/Content-Design-Sarah-Winters/dp/1916323316): By Sarah Winters. Visual and written content have to work together, and this sets the standard for the written half in government.

- [Observing the User Experience: A Practitioner's Guide to User Research](https://www.amazon.com/Observing-User-Experience-Practitioners-Research/dp/0123848695): Goodman, Kuniavsky and Moed. A methods reference for testing visual work with real users.

- [Interviewing Users: How to Uncover Compelling Insights](https://www.amazon.com/Interviewing-Users-Uncover-Compelling-Insights/dp/1933820118): By Steve Portigal. Visual design decisions are stronger when grounded in what users actually said.

- [Smashing UX Design: Foundations for Designing Online User Experiences](https://www.skillsoft.com/book/smashing-ux-design-foundations-for-designing-online-user-experiences-807d15f0-f050-11e6-8ce3-0242c0a80c07): A foundational guide across the design process, including the visual layer.

## Professional development tips

- Set visual design standards across teams and run critique that raises the standard
- Own the visual identity as it applies to services, and its relationship to the GOV.UK design language
- Develop designers deliberately, since visual judgement grows through critique and exposure
- Make accessibility a default of visual design practice rather than a review at the end
- Represent visual design in service assessments and governance with evidence
- Build reusable assets and templates as shared infrastructure
- Protect the time needed for design quality when delivery pressure rises
