
# IT Operations Problem Manager - upskill resources

This page focus on learning and growth resources for the work role of IT operatins problem manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books. 

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement,

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

- For more contact Joel Henderson <joel@joelparkerhenderson.com>

## Coursera courses

- [ITSM Foundations: Optimizing IT Service Management](https://www.coursera.org/learn/itsm-foundations-optimizing-it-service-management): This course explores core principles and real-world applications of IT Service Management (ITSM) through the ServiceNow platform, covering ITIL best practices. By the end, learners will develop hands-on skills in managing service requests, incidents, and problems. No prior experience in ITSM or ServiceNow is required.

- [Advanced ITSM with ServiceNow: Optimizing IT Operations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advanced-itsm-with-servicenow-optimizing-it-operations): This course covers managing incidents, problems, service requests, and knowledge management, as well as leveraging change management and the configuration management database (CMDB). Ideal for IT Operations Managers, IT Professionals, Service Desk Staff, and ITSM Consultants looking to enhance their skills in ServiceNow platform optimization.

- [Root Cause Analysis: Principles and Benefits](https://www.coursera.org/learn/root-cause-analysis-principles-and-benefits): This course introduces Root Cause Analysis (RCA) as a systematic process used to identify the fundamental causes of problems or events, preventing their recurrence. Upon completion, learners will master RCA methodologies, enabling them to enhance operational efficiency, improve product quality, and foster a proactive problem-solving culture.

It provides practical guidance for implementing and optimizing problem management.

- [RCA in IT: Root Cause Analysis for IT Environments](https://www.bmc.com/blogs/root-cause-analysis/): BMC Helix blog article providing essential practices for IT teams, offering steps and best practices for conducting root cause analysis. - [What Is Root Cause Analysis? The Complete RCA Guide](https://www.splunk.com/en_us/blog/learn/root-cause-analysis.html): Splunk's complete guide explaining Root Cause Analysis (RCA) as a structured, data-driven process that identifies fundamental causes of issues within systems or processes. This guide covers multiple RCA techniques including the Pareto chart and Six Sigma methods widely used in IT operations and software development.

- [Virima - Guide to ITIL Incident and Problem Management](https://virima.com/blog/guide-to-itil-incident-and-problem-management): This guide helps ITSM professionals understand the difference between incidents and problems in ITIL 4, including how to move from constant firefighting to proactive prevention. It emphasizes documenting every incident with details like time, affected service, symptoms, and resolution.

- [Atlassian - Problem Management Roles and Responsibilities](https://www.atlassian.com/itsm/problem-management/roles-and-responsibilities): This article explains that the problem owner manages the overall process for a specific problem, coordinating and directing all facets of the problem management effort, including bringing the right teams, tools, and information together.

- [InvGate Blog - The Problem Manager Role: Skills, Responsibilities, and Best Practices](https://blog.invgate.com/problem-manager): Published in January 2025, this blog post explains the Problem Manager's role in ensuring the reliability and continuity of IT services. - [Atlassian - IT Change Management: ITIL Framework & Best Practices](https://www.atlassian.com/itsm/change-management): This resource explains that change management is an IT practice designed to minimize disruptions to IT services while making changes to critical systems and services. It emphasizes how IT change management reduces incidents while keeping processes agile and minimizing work disruptions.

- [Lansweeper - A Beginner's Guide to CMDB in ITSM](https://www.lansweeper.com/blog/cybersecurity/a-beginners-guide-to-cmdb-in-itsm-unlocking-efficiency/): This guide explains that a Configuration Management Database (CMDB) serves as a centralized repository that captures and manages information on all IT assets, or configuration items (CIs), and their interdependencies. A well-maintained CMDB offers several key benefits for ITSM teams.

- [Invensis Learning - A Comprehensive Guide to IT Service Continuity Management](https://www.invensislearning.com/blog/it-service-continuity-management/): This guide covers maintaining IT service continuity plans and IT recovery plans that support overall business continuity plans. It discusses implementing proactive measures to increase service availability and ensuring continuity mechanisms meet agreed-upon business continuity targets.

## Research articles

- [Improving the Software Problem Management Process: A Case Study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11908562_5): This Springer research paper presents a case study from TietoEnator Oyj, examining what challenges are related to the software problem management process. The study provides insights into practical problem management implementation and improvement strategies.

- [Difficulties in Implementing Effective Problem Management](https://www.ijser.org/paper/Difficulties-in-Implementing-Effective-Problem-Management.html): This research paper focuses on the benefits of effective problem management and the barriers in implementing effective problem management. It sets a platform for industry-based research opportunities into the factors which make problem management difficult and how to make this process more effective.

- [A Comprehensive Survey on Root Cause Analysis in (Micro) Services](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00803v1): Published in 2024, this survey provides a comprehensive, structured review of root cause analysis techniques within microservices, exploring methodologies that include metrics, traces, logs, and multi-model data. It covers RCA in the context of Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations (AIOps).

- [Root Cause Analysis in Quality Problem Solving: A Case Study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325207104_Root_cause_analysis_in_quality_problem_solving_of_research_information_systems_A_case_study): This case study examines root cause analysis for quality problem solving in research information systems, identifying the underlying causes of quality deviations, ensuring the right directions of further actions, and preventing quality problem recurrence.

- [An IT Service Management Literature Review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349857851_An_IT_Service_Management_Literature_Review_Challenges_Benefits_Opportunities_and_Implementation_Practices): This comprehensive systematic literature review analyzed 47 articles from top journals and conferences, extracting and critically analyzing the benefits, challenges, opportunities, and practices for ITSM implementation. The research found that most organizations implement ITSM frameworks to better manage their infrastructure and services.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): This study examining 256 organizations found that ITSM capability contributes to IS effectiveness through sustaining the alignment of the IS function with the business, with effects mediated by IS-business alignment and strengthened by IS strategic conservativeness.

- [Minimizing Incident Response Time Using Quantum Computing](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11219-023-09632-6): Published in Software Quality Journal (2023), this research proposes a solution to the incident response problem that acts in a minimal amount of time for real scenarios handling large numbers of incidents using quantum computing approaches.

- [Cyber Incident Response and Planning: A Flexible Approach](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1361372321000099): This 2021 research follows a design science research approach to develop an incident response management maturity model (IRM3) aligned with practice requirements, providing frameworks for improving incident response capabilities.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): This systematic literature review identified 14 challenges and 17 enablers related to different phases of root cause analysis. Challenges include "need for expertise," "employee bias," and "poor data quality," while enablers include "visualization tools," "collaborative platforms," and "machine learning techniques."

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): Published in Information Systems Management, this research paper developed a five-step process improvement framework for ITIL service operations based on the Lean toolset, using a design science research approach to enhance service delivery effectiveness.

## Professional Certifications

- **ITIL 4 Foundation:** Required baseline certification for problem managers
- **ITIL 4 Practitioner: Problem Management:** Specialized certification focusing on problem management practice
- **ITIL 4 Managing Professional:** Advanced certification track for senior problem managers

## Industry Standards

- **ISO/IEC 20000:** International standard for IT service management
- **COBIT:** Framework for IT governance and management

## Professional development tips

- Commit to continuous learning because tools and techniques evolve rapidly
- Build a portfolio of demonstration projects so you can share your skills
- Join relevant communities, attend events, and connect with colleagues

- Seek opportunities to work with diverse people and projects
