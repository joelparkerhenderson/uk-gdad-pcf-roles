# Chief data architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of chief data architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you set the vision for the organisation's use of data, oversee the design of multiple data models, are accountable for aligning to the data strategy, and champion data architecture at the most senior levels across government. The resources below emphasise data strategy, governance leadership, and influence at executive level.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Enterprise Data Integration, Governance and Architecture](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/data-architect-advanced): Covers data strategy and best practice in data management, applying governance, compliance and risk management to secure enterprise data, and designing enterprise-wide data architectures.

- [Strategic Leadership: Influence and Thought Leadership](https://www.coursera.org/learn/strategic-leadership-influence-thought-leadership): Covers influence techniques used by senior leaders including mapping influence networks, framing, building legitimacy through consistency, and leading meaning-making in uncertain moments.

- [Fundamentals of Data Governance](https://www.coursera.org/learn/fundamentals-of-data-governance): Covers the Data Governance Institute framework and DAMA-DMBOK, alongside regulations such as GDPR, giving a common reference point when setting organisational policy.

## Udemy courses

- [Fundamental Course of Data Architecture 2.0 (101 level)](https://www.udemy.com/course/data-architecture-big-data-architecture-data-mesh-database-management/): A broad orientation across data architecture, big data architecture, data mesh and database management. Useful for keeping a strategic overview current across a fast-moving landscape.

- [Data Architecture for Data Engineers: Practical Approaches](https://www.udemy.com/course/data-architecture-for-data-engineers/): Covers warehouses, lakes and lakehouses with practical treatment of modelling, governance and security, keeping strategic direction grounded in what teams can actually build.

- [Enterprise Architecture Practitioner with the TOGAF Standard](https://www.udemy.com/course/togaf-10-practitioner/): Practitioner-level treatment of applying and tailoring TOGAF, including architecture governance, which is directly relevant to establishing a data architecture authority.

## Skillsoft courses

- [Courses for Data Management (DAMA-DMBOK, Intermediate)](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/data-management-517347c0-3fc1-4e3d-871a-ed9b81397617): A channel covering the DAMA knowledge areas including governance, architecture, modelling, storage, security, integration, metadata and quality, providing a common curriculum for a whole team.

- [Courses for Strategic Thinking](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/strategic-thinking-1b49aa30-e719-11e6-9835-f723b46a2688): Covers the vision and persuasion skills strategic leadership requires, which differ from those needed for operational leadership.

## edX courses

- [Enterprise Data Management (Indiana University)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-administration/indiana-university-enterprise-data-management): Covers data strategy and governance, integration, master data management and big data technologies at an enterprise level.

- [15.317 Organizational Leadership and Change](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-317-organizational-leadership-and-change-summer-2009/): A Sloan course blending leadership theory and practice, in which participants reflect on prior leadership experience and apply the lessons to develop further.

- [15.067 Competitive Decision-Making and Negotiation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-067-competitive-decision-making-and-negotiation-spring-2011/): Develops negotiation through integrative bargaining cases between multiple parties over multiple issues, directly relevant to cross-departmental data agreements.

## Blog posts

- [Government Data Architecture blog](https://dataarchitecture.blog.gov.uk/): The UK government data architecture blog covering enterprise and technical principles across departments, and the natural venue for a chief data architect to both read and contribute.

- [Data governance on the Government Data Architecture blog](https://dataarchitecture.blog.gov.uk/category/data-architecture/data-governance/): Coverage of how governance is applied to data architecture in a cross-government setting.

- [Data Standards on the Data in Government blog](https://dataingovernment.blog.gov.uk/category/data-standards/): Posts on cross-government data standards work, including the Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard mandate for central government.

- [Data Fabric vs. Data Mesh](https://www.alation.com/blog/data-mesh-vs-data-fabric/): Separates the decentralised operating model of data mesh from the technology-centric pattern of data fabric, useful when setting direction against vendor pressure.

- [Best Practices for Distributed Domain-Driven Architecture on the Data Mesh](https://www.confluent.io/blog/distributed-domain-driven-architecture-data-mesh-best-practices/): Covers domain-driven data architecture at organisational scale with a real implementation case study.

- [Let's Architect! Modern data architectures](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/lets-architect-modern-data-architectures/): A curated collection on treating data as a product and organising architecture around business domains.

- [Data Governance and Metadata Management: A Vital Duo for Data-Driven Success](https://atlan.com/data-governance-and-metadata-management/): Explains why governance programmes without metadata infrastructure tend to stall, and how to sequence the two.

- [6 Modern Data Architecture Principles: A Fast-Track Guide](https://data.world/blog/modern-data-architecture-principles/): A compact set of principles with reasoning, useful as a starting point when drafting organisational principles.

- [Building a better future: the enterprise architect's role in leading organizational transformation](https://www.cio.com/article/3600078/building-a-better-future-the-enterprise-architects-role-in-leading-organizational-transformation.html): On moving from documenting the organisation to leading change within it, and the credibility that requires.

- [Not Just for the C-Suite: Building the Missing Career Path for Enterprise Architects](https://www.eatransformation.com/p/building-missing-career-path-for-enterprise-architects): Argues for a deliberate architecture career path, relevant when you are accountable for growing the discipline.

## Research articles

- [Data Mesh: A Systematic Gray Literature Review](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3687301): An ACM Computing Surveys review characterising data mesh in terms of design principles, architectural components and organisational roles, giving an evidence base for a strategic decision.

- [Architecting Data-Intensive Applications: From Data Architecture Design to Its Quality Assurance](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.12011): Examines how architecture practice determines data quality outcomes in data-intensive systems.

- [Metadata Management in Data Governance: A Comprehensive Scientific Analysis](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391179319_METADATA_MANAGEMENT_IN_DATA_GOVERNANCE_A_COMPREHENSIVE_SCIENTIFIC_ANALYSIS): A scientific treatment of metadata management within data governance and its organisational effects.

- [Assessing the Role of Metadata in Data Governance Policies](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/390163942_Assessing_the_Role_of_Metadata_in_Data_Governance_Policies): Uses case studies and empirical evidence to show the impact of metadata management on decision making, risk management and regulatory compliance.

- [Improving Data Quality and Data Governance Using Master Data Management: A Review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/354967542_Improving_Data_Quality_and_Data_Governance_Using_Master_Data_Management_A_Review): Reviews how master data management addresses data quality by standardising and profiling data from diverse sources.

- [Data Governance for Master Data Management and Beyond](https://www.academia.edu/27946987/Data_Governance_for_Master_Data_Management_and_Beyond): Uses six mini case studies to evaluate a governance morphology against empirical data.

- [Enterprise Data Modelling Methodologies: A Comparative Analysis of Inmon, Kimball, and Data Vault](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.29355): Compares the dominant enterprise modelling methodologies, useful when setting an organisation-wide standard.

- [Challenges of Government Enterprise Architecture Work: Stakeholders' Views](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-85204-9_31): Concludes from government interviews that the governance level of architecture must be adequately adjusted and enforced as a tool for business operations development.

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Finds that architecture benefits are largely realised indirectly through advisory services within change projects, which has direct implications for how a chief architect positions the function.

- [Evaluating Data Quality: Comparative Insights on Standards, Methodologies, and Modern Software Tools](https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9292/14/15/3038): An open-access comparison of data quality standards, methodologies and tooling, useful when specifying organisational measurement.

## Professional development tips

- Express the data vision in terms of the decisions and services it enables, not the technologies it adopts
- Publish a small number of durable principles and defend them consistently, rather than a large catalogue nobody reads
- Build alliances with the chief data officer, information governance and finance, since data strategy fails on funding and permission more often than on design
- Represent your organisation in cross-government data forums, because interoperability is decided there
- Give your senior architects genuine decision rights, and hold them to published standards rather than to your personal preference
- Keep a live assessment of where the current estate diverges from the target, with cost and risk attached
- Invest in the profession by mentoring, publishing and hiring deliberately, since the capability outlasts any single programme
