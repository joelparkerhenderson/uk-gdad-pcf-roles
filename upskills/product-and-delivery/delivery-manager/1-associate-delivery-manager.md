# Associate delivery manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of associate delivery manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you support delivery for a team with guidance, learning agile practice, facilitation and how to remove blockers. The resources below emphasise agile delivery fundamentals, facilitation and the government service standard.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Agile Project Management (Google)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/agile-project-management): Part of the Google Project Management Certificate, covering Scrum pillars and values, building and managing a product backlog, value-driven delivery and organising Scrum events.

- [Applied Scrum for Agile Project Management (UMD)](https://www.coursera.org/learn/applied-scrum-for-agile-project-management): Covers applying Scrum roles, processes and tools to real team situations, with a strong foundation in agile principles.

- [Agile Project Management: Estimate, Lead and Deliver Value](https://www.coursera.org/learn/agile-project-management-estimate-lead-deliver-value): Covers estimation, leading delivery and connecting delivery activity to value rather than to output.

- [Mastering Agile Project Management and the Scrum Master's Role](https://www.udemy.com/course/mastering-agile-navigating-the-scrum-masters-role/): Explores the role as coach and servant leader, supporting the team and removing impediments while facilitating planning, stand-ups, reviews and retrospectives.

## Blog posts

- [A day in the life of a delivery manager](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2012/12/12/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-delivery-manager/): The Government Digital Service on the delivery manager as servant leader, enabling the team by removing impediments and building an effective working environment.

- [Explaining the role of a Delivery Manager](https://emilywebber.co.uk/what-is-an-agile-delivery-manager/): Emily Webber on what good delivery managers do, including building motivated teams, protecting collaboration, coaching in agile technique and identifying blockers proactively.

- [Making the change from project to delivery management](https://defradigital.blog.gov.uk/2017/09/27/making-the-change-from-project-to-delivery-management/): A first-hand account of moving from project management to delivery management in a government department.

- [What we've learnt about scaling agile](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2012/10/26/what-weve-learnt-about-scaling-agile/): Covers managing risks, issues and blockers with collective responsibility, and running a stand-up of stand-ups across delivery managers.

- [GOV.UK: a journey in scaling agile](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2018/04/26/gov-uk-a-journey-in-scaling-agile/): An honest account of the challenges of scaling agile delivery and how the approach had to adapt.

- [The role of the agile wall at GDS](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2012/12/19/the-agile-wall/): On making work visible so that teams focus and others can see what is happening, still the cheapest delivery management tool available.

- [Agile delivery, GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery): The authoritative guidance covering principles, tools, governance, user stories, planning and the discovery, alpha, beta, live and retirement phases.

- [Agile and government services: an introduction](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/agile-government-services-introduction): Explains why agile is government policy for digital services and what that means for delivery practice.

- [Chapter 11: The governance and management of portfolios](https://projectdelivery.gov.uk/teal-book/home/part-c-managing-portfolios/chapter-11-the-governance-and-management-of-portfolios/): UK government guidance on portfolio governance, from the Teal Book on project delivery.

- [Chapter 12: Managing a portfolio](https://projectdelivery.gov.uk/teal-book/home/part-c-managing-portfolios/chapter-12-managing-a-portfolio/): Covers the practical work of managing a portfolio in a government context, including prioritisation and assurance.

## Research articles

- [Understanding how agile teams reach effectiveness: A systematic literature review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1053482224000469): Takes stock of what the evidence actually shows about what makes agile teams effective, and where the research is still thin.

- [Do Agile Scaling Approaches Make A Difference? An Empirical Comparison of Team Effectiveness Across Popular Scaling Approaches](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06599): Surveys 15,078 members of 4,013 teams and 1,841 stakeholders, finding only minor differences in effectiveness between SAFe, LeSS, Scrum of Scrums and custom approaches.

- [Exploring software development at the very large-scale: a revelatory case study and research agenda for agile method adaptation](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-017-9524-2): Identifies customer involvement, software architecture and inter-team coordination as the key difficulties at large scale.

- [An Empirical Analysis of the Effect of Agile Teams on Software Productivity](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/330191532_An_Empirical_Analysis_of_the_Effect_of_Agile_Teams_on_Software_Productivity): Finds team effectiveness, team management, motivation and customer satisfaction to be the factors team members perceive as most affecting productivity.

- [The Effects of Continuous Integration on Software Development: a Systematic Literature Review](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05451): Analyses 101 empirical studies covering development activities, process, quality assurance and defects.

- [Seven pitfalls to avoid when running controlled experiments on the web](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1557019.1557139): Kohavi and colleagues on the specific errors that make product experiments produce confident but wrong answers.

- [Statistical Challenges in Online Controlled Experiments: A Review of A/B Testing Methodology](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00031305.2023.2257237): A review of the recurring statistical problems in the experiments product teams rely on.

- [How enterprise architecture leads to organisational benefits](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401222000883): Traces how technical and organisational decisions actually translate into outcomes.

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Finds that benefits are realised indirectly within change projects rather than through artefacts, a lesson that transfers directly to roadmaps.

- [Enterprise Architecture and IT Governance to Support the BizDevOps Approach: a Systematic Mapping Study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-024-10473-2): Maps how governance adapts, or fails to adapt, to continuous delivery.

## Professional development tips

- Learn the GOV.UK Service Manual and the delivery phases, since they frame everything you will do
- Make work visible, because a wall or board that everyone reads solves more problems than any process change
- Practise facilitation deliberately, including how to keep a session on track without dominating it
- Learn to spot a blocker early and escalate it clearly rather than waiting for it to resolve itself
- Sit in on other teams' ceremonies to see different ways of running the same events
- Protect the team's focus, since that is the most valuable thing you can offer them
- Ask for feedback from your team on whether you are helping or adding process
