# Business architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of business architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you apply and develop business architecture frameworks in your organisation, advise on strategy implementation, mediate between stakeholders at all levels, and guide other architects. The resources below emphasise advanced practice, strategic design and business change, and building capability in methods and tools.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [TOGAF 10 Foundation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/togaf-10-foundation): Covers the full Architecture Development Method, the Enterprise Continuum, the Architecture Repository and Architecture Capability, plus stakeholder management, risk management and architecture trade-offs. Useful for architects who need to establish or refresh a framework in their organisation.

- [Influencing the Organisation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/influencing-the-organisation-leading-within-and-beyond-the-organisation): Covers managing stakeholders and organisational politics while mobilising people, and aligning your mission with the organisation's purpose. Directly supports the mediation and consensus-building expected at this level.

- [Stakeholder and Strategic Alignment](https://www.coursera.org/learn/stakeholder-and-strategic-alignment): Focuses on identifying stakeholders, understanding their interests and aligning them behind a strategic direction. A practical complement to the modelling side of business architecture.

## Udemy courses

- [Enterprise Architecture Practitioner with the TOGAF Standard](https://www.udemy.com/course/togaf-10-practitioner/): The practitioner-level treatment of TOGAF, covering how to apply the ADM in a live organisation rather than simply describe it. Suited to architects already working with the framework.

- [Enterprise Architecture Modeling Foundation with ArchiMate 3](https://www.udemy.com/course/archimate-3/): Covers the strategy layer, linking capabilities to outcomes, and showing how behaviour elements realise value streams and capabilities. Precision in notation matters more as your models are consumed by others.

- [BIZBOK Exam Prep: 1000+ Business Architecture Questions](https://www.udemy.com/course/bizbok-exam-prep-1000-business-architecture-questions/): Over a thousand practice questions covering the main areas of the Business Architecture Body of Knowledge, aimed at business architects, business analysts and enterprise architects preparing for certification.

## Skillsoft courses

- [TOGAF Advanced: Enterprise Architecture](https://www.skillsoft.com/courses/5557912-togaf-advanced-enterprise-architecture/): An in-depth explanation of the TOGAF framework, covering how to enhance and mature architectures using the framework. Aimed at practitioners responsible for the maturity of a practice, not just its outputs.

- [Courses for Strategic Thinking](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/strategic-thinking-1b49aa30-e719-11e6-9835-f723b46a2688): A channel covering the vision and persuasion skills strategic leadership requires, which differ from the skills of day-to-day operational leadership.

- [Enterprise Business Architecture: The Formal Link between Strategy and Results (book)](https://www.skillsoft.com/book/enterprise-business-architecture-the-formal-link-between-strategy-and-results-78c22a70-efa2-11e6-9877-0242c0a80506): Sets out how to develop a formal but pragmatic enterprise business architecture, and challenges the reader to analyse the architectural needs of their own organisation.

## edX courses

- [Introduction: Emerging Technologies in an Enterprise Environment (UBCx)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/university-of-british-columbia-introduction-emerging-technologies-in-an-enterprise-environment): Covers business architecture development and The Open Group Business Architecture, and explains an organisation's enterprise architecture in the context of TOGAF standards including technology, data and application domains.

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (Institute of Science Tokyo)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/institute-of-science-tokyo-introduction-to-business-architecture-3): Uses the DEMO methodology and Enterprise Ontology to map business architecture with unusual rigour. Valuable when you need to settle definitional arguments with a formal method.

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (TokyoTechX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-administration/tokyo-institute-of-technology-introduction-to-business-architecture): The TokyoTechX run of the same material on enterprise ontology and business architecture mapping.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.320 Strategic Organizational Design](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-320-strategic-organizational-design-spring-2011/): Covers functional, divisional and matrix organisations, how IT creates new organisational possibilities, and innovative forms such as democratic decision making and crowd-based organisations. Broadens the design options you can put in front of executives.

- [15.769 Operations Strategy](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-769-operations-strategy-fall-2010/pages/calendar/): Covers enterprise and value chain architecture alongside operations strategy, giving a rigorous basis for reasoning about how work is organised at scale.

- [15.571 Generating Business Value from Information Technology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-571-generating-business-value-from-information-technology-spring-2009/syllabus/): Uses case studies including UPS, Merrill Lynch, 7-Eleven Japan and Pacific Life to examine how organisations choose an operating model balancing business process integration and standardisation.

## Blog posts

- [The Business Architect's toolbox: Value Stream Mapping](https://bizzdesign.com/blog/business-architect-toolbox-stream-mapping): Explains how visualising value flow across processes, teams and systems identifies inefficiencies, aligns capabilities and drives strategic improvement.

- [Capabilities and Value Streams: Business Architecture's Essential Alliance](https://www.bpminstitute.org/resources/articles/capabilities-value-streams-business-architectures-essential-alliance/): Argues that marrying capability with value stream is the lynchpin of business architecture success, supporting root cause analysis, strategic planning, funding allocation and executive communication.

- [Business Architecture Essentials: Aligning your Capabilities, Processes and Business Information](https://processrenewal.com/business-architecture-essentials-aligning-capabilities-processes-business-information-connecting-dots/): Connects capability, process and information models and shows how to keep them coherent as the practice scales.

- [Business Architect Role: Skills, Deliverables, and Value](https://capstera.com/knowledge-hub/articles/business-architect): Describes the deliverable set including capability maps, value stream maps, capability heatmaps, target operating model designs and capability-based roadmaps linking strategic goals to funded initiatives.

- [The Power of Enterprise Architecture Operating Model](https://www.boc-group.com/en/blog/ea/operating-models-in-business-transformation/): Explains how operating models are used to structure business transformation and how architecture teams contribute to them.

- [Building a better future: the enterprise architect's role in leading organizational transformation](https://www.cio.com/article/3600078/building-a-better-future-the-enterprise-architects-role-in-leading-organizational-transformation.html): Examines how architects move from documenting the organisation to leading change within it.

- [Architecting the Future: Enterprise Architecture Strategies for Agile and Resilient Organizations](https://www.rsaconference.com/library/blog/architecting-the-future-enterprise-architecture-strategies-for-agile-and-resilient-organizations): Discusses how architecture practice adapts to organisations that need to be both agile and resilient.

- [SAFe and Business Architecture](https://scaledagile.com/blog/safe-and-business-architecture/): Explains how business architecture connects to scaled agile planning cycles, which matters when architecture outputs need to shape funded increments of work.

- [5 steps for building collaboration into an enterprise architecture revamp](https://www.redhat.com/architect/architecture-refresh-leadership): Practical guidance on integrating community federation and collaborative working into architecture practice while staying consistent with purpose, vision and values.

- [Customer Journeys, Value Streams and Business Capabilities, and how to bring them together](https://scottmillett.medium.com/customer-journeys-value-streams-and-business-capabilities-and-how-to-bring-them-together-7eb912937e23): Untangles three commonly conflated artefacts and shows how to use them together without duplicated effort.

## Research articles

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Finds that many architecture benefits are intangible and realised indirectly within business change projects, and that it is the advisory services enabled by the architecture that provide the benefit.

- [TOGAF-based Enterprise Architecture Practice: An Exploratory Case Study](https://aisel.aisnet.org/cais/vol43/iss1/20/): Shows that organisations adapt EA methodologies rather than adopting them as published, and that real practices differ substantially from the documented methodology. Important reading before mandating a framework.

- [The theoretical basis of enterprise architecture: A critical review and taxonomy of relevant theories](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0268396220977873): Kotusev and Kurnia review and classify the theories used to explain architecture practice, giving a stronger conceptual footing for advocacy.

- [Nuanced Responses to Enterprise Architecture Management: Loyalty, Voice, and Exit](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07421222.2018.1550549): Examines how people in organisations actually respond to architecture management, using loyalty, voice and exit as a lens. Useful for understanding resistance.

- [Stakeholder Perception of Enterprise Architecture](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/220757098_Stakeholder_Perception_of_Enterprise_Architecture): Finds stakeholders often perceive architecture management as abstract and hard to comprehend, and links governance processes and communication to stakeholder values.

- [How enterprise architecture leads to organisational benefits](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401222000883): Traces the mechanisms connecting architecture work to organisational outcomes, published in the International Journal of Information Management.

- [Modeling Business Capabilities in Enterprise Architecture Practice: The Case of Business Capability Models](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/372098424_Modeling_Business_Capabilities_in_Enterprise_Architecture_Practice_The_Case_of_Business_Capability_Models): Identifies nine general business capability modelling approaches used for aligning business and IT, and compares their purposes.

- [Designing Consistent Business Capability Models](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-94590-8_28): Presents a design science method for aligning value streams, capabilities and information concepts, addressing a known gap in systematic alignment methods.

- [Challenges of Government Enterprise Architecture Work: Stakeholders' Views](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-85204-9_31): Based on 21 interviews in Finnish state government, concludes that the governance level of enterprise architecture must be adequately adjusted and enforced as a tool for business operations development.

- [Enterprise Architecture and IT Governance to Support the BizDevOps Approach: a Systematic Mapping Study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-024-10473-2): A mapping study of 86 primary and 11 secondary studies, noting a lack of empirical validation and a prevalence of recommendation-oriented papers in the field.

- [Business Architecture: A Comprehensive Guide](https://us.amazon.com/Business-Architecture-comprehensive-Jonathan-Hunsley-ebook/dp/B0D2V1F5N5): A practitioner guide to directing strategically aligned, customer centric and outcome focused change.

- [Business Architecture: The Enabler of Business Strategy](https://us.amazon.com/dp/B0D8C7VWYQ): Samuel Holcman's methodology from the Business Architecture Center of Excellence.

- [Transformed: Moving to the Product Operating Model](https://www.amazon.com/Transformed-Moving-Product-Operating-Silicon-ebook/dp/B0CXG97J55): Cagan and colleagues on shifting an organisation to a product operating model, a target state frequently requested in public sector transformation.

- [Essential Strategy and Enterprise Architecture](https://www.amazon.com/Essential-Strategy-Enterprise-Architecture-Haloedscape/dp/1507840144): A compact treatment of the link between strategy formulation and architecture practice.

## Professional development tips

- Invest in the credibility of the practice, not only the quality of the artefacts, because adoption is the harder problem
- Learn to run a difficult conversation between two senior stakeholders without taking a side prematurely
- Publish reusable patterns and standards so that others can work without you in the room
- Track which architecture outputs actually changed a decision, and retire the ones that never do
- Mentor at least one junior architect deliberately, because teaching exposes gaps in your own reasoning
- Build relationships with finance and portfolio colleagues, since capability-based roadmaps only bite when they shape funding
- Read empirical research on architecture practice, not only framework documentation, to stay honest about what works
