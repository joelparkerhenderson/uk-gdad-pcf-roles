# Trainee business architect - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of trainee business architect.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are building foundational knowledge of business architecture frameworks, principles and techniques, supporting more senior architects, and learning to communicate clearly with stakeholders. The resources below emphasise fundamentals, vocabulary, and supported practice.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [TOGAF 10 Foundation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/togaf-10-foundation): A structured journey through the core concepts of enterprise architecture, including the BDAT domains, the Architecture Development Method (ADM), architecture governance principles, the Enterprise Continuum and the Architecture Repository. A good first framework course for a trainee who needs the shared vocabulary of the profession.

- [IBM Systems and Solutions Architect Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/ibm-systems-and-solutions-architect): A multi-course programme covering architectural thinking, requirements, architecture decisions and documentation. Useful for a trainee business architect who wants to understand how business architecture connects to the wider architecture disciplines.

- [Enterprise Data Architecture and Operations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/enterprise-data-architecture-and-operations): Covers enterprise architecture principles including the TOGAF and Zachman frameworks, alongside data modernisation and lifecycle management. Helps a trainee see how the information dimension of an organisation is described and governed.

## Udemy courses

- [What is Enterprise Architecture, and why do we need it?](https://www.udemy.com/course/what-is-enterprise-architecture/): A short orientation course that explains what enterprise architecture is for, what problems it solves, and where business architecture sits within it. A sensible starting point before tackling framework-heavy material.

- [Business Architecture Part 1](https://www.udemy.com/course/business-architecture-part-1/): An introduction to the practice of business architecture, its artefacts and its purpose within an organisation. Aimed at people new to the discipline.

- [Business Architecture Foundation with the TOGAF Standard](https://www.udemy.com/course/togaf-10-ba-foundation/): Covers how to define what a business does through a TOGAF 10 aligned business capability map, including naming, description, layering, heat maps and value-stream mapping. Includes over 200 pages of course material, quiz questions and practice tests.

## Skillsoft courses

- [Architecture & Enterprise Governance (COBIT 5)](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/architecture-enterprise-governance-d4ce0c78-292d-11e7-9844-0242c0a8080b): A beginner-level course introducing the relationship between architecture and enterprise governance. Helps a trainee understand why architecture decisions need governance and where governance sits in an organisation.

- [Courses for DDAT Enterprise Architect](https://www.skillsoft.com/journey/ddat-enterprise-architect-9cfc71da-ce69-4df8-b22b-fd3f0ac06429): A curated learning journey aligned to the UK Government Digital and Data profession capability framework. Particularly relevant for UK public sector architects at any level.

- [Business Architecture: A Comprehensive Guide (book)](https://www.skillsoft.com/book/business-architecture-a-comprehensive-guide-60733157-6c02-4835-8b8a-535f1b85e285): Available in the Skillsoft library, this practitioner guide provides techniques and guidance to direct strategically aligned, customer centric and outcome focused change. Good background reading while shadowing senior architects.

## edX courses

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (Institute of Science Tokyo)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/institute-of-science-tokyo-introduction-to-business-architecture-3): Teaches the concepts of mapping a business architecture using the DEMO (Design and Engineering Methodology for Organizations) methodology as explained in Enterprise Ontology. A rigorous, methodical introduction to modelling an organisation.

- [Introduction to Business Architecture (TokyoTechX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-administration/tokyo-institute-of-technology-introduction-to-business-architecture): The earlier TokyoTechX run of the same material, covering business architecture mapping concepts and enterprise ontology fundamentals.

- [Introduction: Emerging Technologies in an Enterprise Environment (UBCx)](https://www.edx.org/learn/business-management/university-of-british-columbia-introduction-emerging-technologies-in-an-enterprise-environment): Module 1 covers business architecture with an overview of the business enterprise environment, business architecture development and The Open Group Business Architecture. Also introduces enterprise architecture standards and terminology in the context of TOGAF.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.571 Generating Business Value from Information Technology](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/sloan-school-of-management/15-571-generating-business-value-from-information-technology-spring-2009/syllabus/): An integrative course covering business strategy, finance and organisations, focused on how IT enables fundamental business operating models. Case studies include UPS, Merrill Lynch, 7-Eleven Japan and Pacific Life, examining business process integration and standardisation.

- [15.320 Strategic Organizational Design](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-320-strategic-organizational-design-spring-2011/): Covers effective organisational design in traditional and innovative organisations, including when to use functional, divisional or matrix organisations and how IT creates new organisational possibilities. Directly relevant to understanding the organisational dimension of business architecture.

- [15.990 Architecture and Communication in Organizations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-990-architecture-and-communication-in-organizations-fall-2003/): Examines how organisational architecture shapes communication patterns. Useful background for a trainee learning to interpret how information really flows in an organisation, as opposed to how the org chart says it does.

## Blog posts

- [The Business Architect's Toolbox: An Introduction](https://bizzdesign.com/blog/the-business-architects-toolbox-an-introduction/): An orientation to the core tools a business architect uses day to day, and when each one applies. A good map of the territory for someone new to the role.

- [Introduction to BIZBOK](https://www.bmc.com/blogs/bizbok-introduction/): Explains the Business Architecture Body of Knowledge, its structure and its main domains. Helpful for understanding the reference standard many business architecture teams work from.

- [Business Architect: Skill Sets, Career Paths and Must-Haves](https://www.leanix.net/en/wiki/ea/business-architect): Describes the skill set combining strategic analysis, systems thinking and modelling techniques such as capability mapping, value stream mapping and process modelling, and explains why communication skills matter most.

- [The Role of a Business Architect in Today's Organizations](https://www.bpminstitute.org/resources/articles/the-role-of-a-business-architect-in-todays-organizations/): Sets out what business architects actually do, the deliverables they own, and how the role differs from business analysis and enterprise architecture.

- [Business Architect Job Description: Roles and Skills](https://www.bacoe.org/business-architect-job-description): From the Business Architecture Center of Excellence, this describes typical responsibilities and required competencies. Useful for a trainee planning a development path.

- [Capabilities and Value Streams: Business Architecture's Essential Alliance](https://www.bpminstitute.org/resources/articles/capabilities-value-streams-business-architectures-essential-alliance/): Explains how the capability map and value stream are used together for root cause analysis, strategic planning, funding allocation and executive communication.

- [How to Create a Business Capability Map, Examples and Templates](https://www.avolutionsoftware.com/our-resources/how-to-create-a-business-capability-map/): A practical, step-by-step walkthrough of building a capability map with worked examples and templates. Good for supported first attempts at the core artefact of the discipline.

- [Business value streams and capabilities](https://bacoach.nl/2020/11/business-value-streams-and-capabilities/): A concise practitioner explanation of the two central concepts and how they relate, written in accessible language.

- [Capability Modeling Crash Course, Part 1](https://medium.com/analysts-corner/capability-modeling-crash-course-part-1-7eb2334f6bfe): A hands-on introduction to building capability models, aimed at analysts moving toward architecture work.

- [The Value Mindset: Demystifying the Business Architecture Value Stream](https://bizarchmastery.com/straighttalk/value-mindset-demystifying-business-architecture-value-stream): Part of the StraightTalk series, this piece strips the jargon out of value streams and explains what they are actually for.

## Research articles

- [A theory building study of enterprise architecture practices and benefits](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10796-014-9542-1): A survey study with 293 respondents developing a model showing how enterprise architecture practices and intermediate benefits jointly help organisations. Finds that EA does not deliver benefits directly, but operates through intermediate results, most notably compliance with EA and architectural insight.

- [A theory building study of enterprise architecture practices and benefits (preprint)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.08112): The open-access preprint of the above, useful for readers without journal access.

- [Dynamic enterprise architecture capabilities and organizational benefits: an empirical mediation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.10036): An empirical study examining how dynamic EA capabilities mediate the relationship between architecture work and organisational benefits.

- [Enterprise Architecture and Organizational Benefits: A Case Study](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/12/19/8237): An open-access case study in Sustainability tracing how architecture practice produced measurable organisational outcomes in a specific setting.

- [How enterprise architecture leads to organisational benefits](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401222000883): Published in the International Journal of Information Management, this study traces the mechanisms linking architecture work to organisational outcomes.

- [Achieving benefits with enterprise architecture](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0963868716302360): Published in the Journal of Strategic Information Systems, this paper argues that many EA benefits are intangible and achieved indirectly within business change projects, and that it is the advisory services enabled by EA, rather than the EA itself, that provide benefits.

- [Achieving Enterprise Architecture Benefits: What Makes the Difference?](https://tunguska.home.xs4all.nl/Publications/Docs/Achieving%20Enterprise%20Architecture%20Benefits%20-%20Steenbergen,%20Foorthuis.pdf): A freely available paper by van Steenbergen and Foorthuis examining the conditions under which architecture practices actually deliver value.

- [The theoretical basis of enterprise architecture: A critical review and taxonomy of relevant theories](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/0268396220977873): Kotusev and Kurnia review the theoretical foundations of the field and offer a taxonomy of the theories used to explain architecture practice.

- [Designing Consistent Business Capability Models: A Method for Aligning Value Streams, Capabilities, and Information Concepts](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-94590-8_28): A design science paper presenting a method for ensuring consistency across business architecture models, supporting more effective decision making and organisational adaptability.

- [Challenges of Government Enterprise Architecture Work: Stakeholders' Views](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-540-85204-9_31): An empirical study based on 21 interviews within Finnish state government, concluding that the governance level of enterprise architecture needs to be adequately adjusted and enforced as a tool for business operations development. Directly relevant to public sector architects.

## Books on Amazon

- [Enterprise Architecture As Strategy: Creating a Foundation for Business Execution](https://www.amazon.com/Enterprise-Architecture-Strategy-Foundation-Execution-ebook/dp/B004OC07EE): By Jeanne W. Ross, Peter Weill and David Robertson. Argues that when it comes to executing strategy, your enterprise architecture may matter far more than your strategy itself. Introduces the operating model concept that underpins much modern business architecture.

- [Business Architecture: A Comprehensive Guide](https://us.amazon.com/Business-Architecture-comprehensive-Jonathan-Hunsley-ebook/dp/B0D2V1F5N5): By Jonathan Hunsley, Debra Paul and colleagues. A practical guide to aligning strategy with execution, equipping readers to design and implement solutions that deliver real-world results.

- [Business Architecture: The Missing Link in Strategy Formulation, Implementation and Execution](https://www.amazon.com/Business-Architecture-Formulation-Implementation-Execution/dp/0994931905): By Pierre Hadaya and Bernard Gagnon. A practical handbook for bridging the gap between formulating, implementing and executing an organisation's strategy.

- [The Practice of Enterprise Architecture: A Modern Approach to Business and IT Alignment](https://www.amazon.com/Practice-Enterprise-Architecture-Approach-Alignment/dp/0645082538): By Svyatoslav Kotusev. Based on empirical research into how architecture is actually practised in organisations, rather than how frameworks say it should be.

- [Business Architecture: The Enabler of Business Strategy](https://us.amazon.com/dp/B0D8C7VWYQ): By Samuel Holcman. A methodology from the Business Architecture Center of Excellence, focused on how business architecture enables strategy execution.

- [Operating Model Canvas: aligning operations and organization with strategy](https://www.amazon.com/Operating-Model-Canvas-Haren-Publishing/dp/9401800715): A practical framework for defining a target operating model covering work processes, organisational structure and supporting systems.

- [Business Model Generation: A Handbook for Visionaries, Game Changers, and Challengers](https://www.amazon.com/Business-Model-Generation-Visionaries-Challengers-ebook/dp/B06X426D4F): By Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur. The canonical guide to the Business Model Canvas, a technique many business architects use to frame an organisation before modelling it in detail.

- [Essential Strategy and Enterprise Architecture](https://www.amazon.com/Essential-Strategy-Enterprise-Architecture-Haloedscape/dp/1507840144): A compact treatment of the relationship between strategy formulation and architecture practice.

- [Execution Architecture: The System Between Strategy and Results](https://www.amazon.com/Execution-Architecture-Between-Strategy-Results-ebook/dp/B0GYPZCZKQ): By Bruce McCullough. A leadership framework for understanding why strategy under-converts and what must change for execution to become clearer, stronger and more durable.

- [Transformed: Moving to the Product Operating Model](https://www.amazon.com/Transformed-Moving-Product-Operating-Silicon-ebook/dp/B0CXG97J55): By Marty Cagan and colleagues. Describes how organisations shift to a product operating model, a target state many public sector business architects are asked to help design.

## Professional development tips

- Learn the vocabulary first, because capability, value stream, process and service have precise meanings that stakeholders often blur
- Ask to shadow senior architects in stakeholder workshops, then write up what you observed and check it with them
- Practise on a small, well-bounded business area before attempting an enterprise-wide view
- Keep a personal glossary of your organisation's terms and how they map to framework terms
- Read your organisation's published strategy and try to draw the capability map it implies
- Join a business architecture community of practice and attend its sessions even when you are only listening
- Ask for feedback on your artefacts early and often, since clarity matters more than completeness at this stage
