# Lead performance analyst - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead performance analyst.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you lead performance analysis across several services, set standards, and represent measurement in service assessments and governance. The resources below emphasise leadership, standards and organisational influence.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Strategic Leadership: Impact, Change, and Decision-Making](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/strategic-leadership-impact-change-decision-making): A Dartmouth Tuck specialisation with Sydney Finkelstein covering the mistakes smart executives make, the neuroscience behind effective decision making, and how to manage and support the people around you.

- [Strategic Leadership: Influence and Thought Leadership](https://www.coursera.org/learn/strategic-leadership-influence-thought-leadership): Covers high-level influence techniques used by senior leaders, including mapping influence networks, strong framing, building legitimacy through consistency, and leading meaning-making in uncertain or crisis moments.

- [Influencing the Organisation](https://www.coursera.org/learn/influencing-the-organisation-leading-within-and-beyond-the-organisation): Covers managing stakeholders and organisational politics while mobilising people, and aligning your mission with the organisation's purpose.

## Udemy courses

- [Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Become a Web Analytics Specialist](https://www.udemy.com/course/google-analytics-ga4-gtm-for-beginners/): Covers GA4 and Google Tag Manager from the basics, including integration with related Google products and analysing reports to reach actionable conclusions.

- [A/B Testing and Experimentation for Beginners](https://www.udemy.com/course/ab-testing-and-experimentation-for-websites-and-marketing/): Covers aligning teams with objectives, defining goals and metrics, developing test ideas, running experiments and analysing results.

- [Product Management: Concise Guide to Product Analytics](https://www.udemy.com/course/product-analytics/): Covers active usage, core and casual use, retention and growth metrics, with a deep dive into identifying good A/B test candidates and building a test plan.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.317 Organizational Leadership and Change](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-317-organizational-leadership-and-change-summer-2009/): A Sloan course blending leadership theory and practice, in which participants reflect on prior leadership experience and apply the lessons to develop their capability further.

- [15.067 Competitive Decision-Making and Negotiation](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-067-competitive-decision-making-and-negotiation-spring-2011/): Develops negotiation skill through integrative bargaining cases between two and more than two parties over multiple issues.

- [Data you must publish, GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/measuring-success/data-you-must-publish): The mandatory measurement requirements for UK government services, covering cost per transaction, user satisfaction, completion rate and digital take-up.

- [Government KPIs: Setting Measurable Goals in the Public Sector](https://www.govpilot.com/blog/government-kpis-setting-measurable-goals-in-the-public-sector): Covers how public sector KPIs demonstrate performance and accountability to stakeholders, and common pitfalls in setting them.

- [The Ultimate Guide to Public Policy KPIs and Metrics](https://www.numberanalytics.com/blog/ultimate-public-policy-kpis-metrics): A structured treatment of indicators for policy contexts, where outcomes are slow and attribution is hard.

- [6 lessons academic research tells us about making data visualizations](https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2015/6-lessons-academic-research-tells-us-about-making-data-visualizations/): Summarises the perception evidence relevant to designing performance dashboards people actually read correctly.

## Research articles

- [Seven pitfalls to avoid when running controlled experiments on the web](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1557019.1557139): Kohavi and colleagues on the specific errors that make web experiments produce confident but wrong answers, drawn from running experiments at scale.

- [Statistical Challenges in Online Controlled Experiments: A Review of A/B Testing Methodology](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00031305.2023.2257237): A review by Larsen, Stallrich, Sengupta, Deng, Kohavi and Stevens covering the statistical problems that recur in online experimentation.

- [Statistical Challenges in Online Controlled Experiments (preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.11366): The open-access version of the above review.

- [Online Controlled Experiments and A/B Testing](https://link.springer.com/rwe/10.1007/978-1-4899-7687-1_891): Kohavi and Longbotham's reference entry defining the method, its assumptions and its limits.

- [Beyond Normality: Reliable A/B Testing with Non-Gaussian Data](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.23666): Addresses the common case where the metric distribution breaks the assumptions of the standard test.

- [A nonparametric sequential test for online randomized experiments](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.02490): Covers testing without fixing the sample size in advance, which matters when stakeholders want to stop early.

- [The Science of Visual Data Communication: What Works](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/15291006211051956): Synthesises the evidence on what makes a visualisation communicate accurately, directly applicable to dashboard design.

- [A Survey of Perception-Based Visualization Studies by Task](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.07477): Organises the perception literature by analytical task, which is the useful way to choose an encoding.

- [An introduction to flexible methods for policy evaluation](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.00641): Covers modern approaches to estimating policy effects when a randomised design is not available.

- [Leakage and the reproducibility crisis in machine-learning-based science](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10499856/): Documents how methodological leakage produces overoptimistic results, a warning that applies to any predictive performance claim.

## Books on Amazon

- [Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments: A Practical Guide to A/B Testing](https://www.amazon.com/Trustworthy-Online-Controlled-Experiments-Practical-ebook/dp/B0845Y3DJV): By Ron Kohavi, Diane Tang and Ya Xu. - [Data Visualization: A Practical Introduction](https://www.amazon.com/Data-Visualization-Introduction-Kieran-Healy/dp/0691181624): By Kieran Healy. Grounds visualisation choices in perception research and works through them in R.

## Professional development tips

- Set the measurement standard across several services and review consistently against it
- Decide where analytical effort is best spent and be willing to stop low-value reporting
- Build an experimentation capability rather than running experiments one at a time
- Develop others deliberately, since your largest effect at this level is through the analysts you raise
- Represent measurement in service assessments and governance so that expectations stay realistic
- Work with data governance and privacy colleagues on what monitoring is appropriate
- Make findings visible across the organisation rather than only within the service team
